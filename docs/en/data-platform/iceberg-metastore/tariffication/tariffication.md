## Billable

- CPU — per core.
- RAM — per 1 GB of RAM.
- Disks — per 1 GB of disk space; the price depends on the disk type (SSD or High-IOPS SSD).
- IP addresses and load balancers, see the [Virtual networks](/en/networks/vnet/tariffication) section for details.
- System disks.

## Not billable

- Outbound and inbound traffic.
- Use of DP Console.
