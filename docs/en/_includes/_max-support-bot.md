{includetag(registration_max_bot)}
1. In the left side menu, select **Registration in the chat bot**.
1. Specify the phone number linked to your MAX account. When entering the number, use the format: `+7XXXXXXXXXXX`.
1. Check the **I agree to use the portal data via the MAX bot** option and click **Register**.

    A successful registration message will be displayed.

1. Open MAX and enter `@vk_tech_support_bot` in the search bar.
1. Go to the **VK Tech Support** bot page.
1. To start a dialog with the bot, click **Start** or enter the `/start` command in the input field.
1. On the right in the input field, click the button to send the bot your phone number.
1. Confirm sending the number.

    The bot will send a PIN code to the email linked to your account.

1. Specify the received PIN code in the chat with the bot.

    Registration is complete.
   {/includetag}

   {includetag(create_request_max_bot)}
1. On the **VK Tech Support** bot page in MAX, select **New request** from the menu or enter the `/new` command in the input field.
1. Select the desired product from the list of available ones.
1. Briefly describe the issue (no more than 60 characters). This text will be used as the request title.
1. Describe the issue in detail.
1. Send the bot a file, photo, or video to be attached to the request, or select the **Leave empty** option.
1. To confirm the request creation, enter `Yes` in the input field.

    The bot will register the request on the VK Tech technical support portal and display its number and content in the chat.

{/includetag}
