# {heading(Certificates, licenses and attestations for VK LLC)[id=it-security-vk-certificates]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

Below is a list of certificates, licenses and attestations of the {var(cloud)} platform, issued by VK LLC, with an indication of compliance with regulatory documents.

The full list of certificates, licenses and attestations is provided on the [Certificates and Licenses](https://cloud.vk.ru/cloud-platform/certificates/) page.

{note:info}
For all questions related to suspected infringement of intellectual property rights, please contact [technical support](/en/contacts).
{/note}

## {heading(Attestation of compliance with information security requirements)[id=it-security-vk-certificates-certificate]}

**Attestation No. 0437.00015.2022 dated March 31, 2022.**

It ensures compliance with the following regulatory documents:

- Federal Law No. 149-FZ dated July 27, 2006 "On Information, Information Technologies and Information Protection".
- Federal Law No. 152-FZ dated July 27, 2006 "On Personal Data".
- Decree of the Government of the Russian Federation No. 1119 dated November 1, 2012 "On Approval of Requirements for the Protection of Personal Data During Their Processing in Personal Data Information Systems".
- Order of the FSTEC of Russia No. 21 dated February 18, 2013 "On Approval of the Composition and Content of Organizational and Technical Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Data During Their Processing in Personal Data Information Systems".

## {heading(Statement of compliance with the Bank of Russia requirements)[id=it-security-vk-certificates-bank-requirements-compliance]}

**Statement on the assessment of compliance with the information protection requirements established by the Bank of Russia standard GOST 57580.1-2017 up to the third level of compliance.**

It ensures compliance with the following regulatory documents:

- Federal Law No. 149-FZ dated July 27, 2006 "On Information, Information Technologies and Information Protection".
- Regulation of the Bank of Russia No. 684-P (level `Standard`).
- Basic standard for insurance organizations performing operations on the financial market (approved by the Bank of Russia, Protocol No. КФНП-24 dated August 9, 2018).
- GOST R 57580.1-2017 "National Standard of the Russian Federation. Security of financial (banking) operations. Protection of information of financial organizations. Basic composition of organizational and technical measures" (compliance level `0.85`).

## {heading(Data center certificates)[id=it-security-vk-certificates-dc-certificates]}

**Certificates of compliance with the TIER-III level of the {var(cloud)} data processing centers.**

They ensure compliance with the instructions of the Bank of Russia No. 4191-U dated November 14, 2016 "On requirements for ensuring uninterrupted and continuous functioning of official websites of insurers and the professional association of insurers on the information and telecommunication network 'Internet' for the purpose of concluding compulsory insurance contracts in the form of electronic documents".
