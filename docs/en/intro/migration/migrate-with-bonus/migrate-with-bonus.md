# {heading(Migration with support from {var(cloud)} specialists)[id=migration-migrate-with-bonus]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

You can migrate resources to {var(cloud)} with support from {var(cloud)} specialists.

Contact your {var(cloud)} customer success manager for a consultation. They will provide information about the migration and help you choose the right infrastructure option for your needs.

## {heading(How the migration works)[id=migration-migrate-with-bonus-how]}

1. _Planning_. Together with your technical experts, {var(cloud)} managers will:

   - design an efficient infrastructure;
   - create a step-by-step migration strategy.
   
1. _Migration process_. {var(cloud)} specialists will transfer your resources to the platform.
1. _Testing_. You will verify that everything works correctly.

After a successful migration, you will receive up to 50000 {linkto(../../../intro/billing/concepts/balance#billing-balance-bonuses)[text=bonuses]} credited to your management console. Additionally, for 30 days, along with [basic technical support](/en/start/support/support-info), you will have access to individual consultations directly from {var(cloud)} migration specialists.

You can submit a request on the [152-FZ Cloud](https://cloud.vk.ru/solutions/152-fz/) page.
