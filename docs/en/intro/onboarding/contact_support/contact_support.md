# {heading(Contact technical support)[id=onboarding-contact-support]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

Technical support is available 24/7. Available communication channels:

- VK Tech technical support portal https://support.mcs.mail.ru;
- [MAX bot](/en/start/support/chatbots/support-bot) `@vk_tech_support_bot`;
- [Telegram bot](/en/start/support/chatbots/support-bot) `@vk_tech_support_bot`;
- email [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru);
- phone +7 (495) 740 05 60.

{note:info}
To speed up the processing of your request, attach your account details (login, project name) to it and describe your issue in as much detail as possible. If possible, add any available diagnostic information: screenshots, logs, or other materials.
{/note}

Instructions on working with the services and the principles of their operation are described in the [documentation](/en). Answers to users' frequently asked questions about the platform are provided in the [FAQ](/en/start/faq) section. You can also use the help of the [AI consultant](/en/start/support/chatbots/ai-bot).

## {heading(Contacting via the technical support portal)[id=onboarding-contact-support-portal]}

1. [Go](https://support.mcs.mail.ru/) to the VK Tech technical support portal.
1. Authenticate using your {var(cloud)} account; to do this, click the **Sign in with {var(cloud)}** button.
1. Click the **Create request** button.
1. In the **Service type** field, select **VK Cloud**.
1. In the remaining fields, select the values that best match the issue you are facing.
   
   The request form will open.

1. In the **Subject** field, enter the subject of your request.
1. Describe the problem or formulate your question in the **Description** field.
1. Fill in the other fields, if any.
1. Attach files that can demonstrate the problem or supplement the question, for example, screenshots, logs, or other materials.
1. Click **Submit**.
   
   The created request will appear in the **My requests** section.

To use the AI assistant in MAX or Telegram, sign up with it.

1. Authenticate using your {var(cloud)} account; to do this, click the **Sign in with VK Cloud** button.
