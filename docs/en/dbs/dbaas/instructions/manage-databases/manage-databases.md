## {heading(Creating a DB)[id=dbaas-manage-databases-create]}

1. {ifdef(public)}[Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/){/ifdef}{ifndef(public)}{linkto(../../../../tools-for-using-services/account/instructions/lk-entry#tools-account-lk-entry)[text=Go to]}{/ifndef} the {var(cloud)} personal dashboard.
1. Navigate to the **Databases** → **Database instances** section.
1. Click on the name of the DB instance.
1. Go to the **Database list** tab.
1. Click the **Add** button.
1. Enter the DB name.
1. Click the **Create database** button.

## {heading(Deleting a DB)[id=dbaas-manage-databases-delete]}

1. {ifdef(public)}[Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/){/ifdef}{ifndef(public)}{linkto(../../../../tools-for-using-services/account/instructions/lk-entry#tools-account-lk-entry)[text=Go to]}{/ifndef} the {var(cloud)} personal dashboard.
1. Navigate to the **Databases** → **Database instances** section.
1. Click on the name of the DB instance.
1. Go to the **Database list** tab.
1. Check the boxes for the databases you want to delete.
1. Click the **Delete** button above the database list.
1. Confirm the deletion.
