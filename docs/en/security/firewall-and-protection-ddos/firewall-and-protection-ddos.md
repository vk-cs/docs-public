## {heading(What is it)[id=firewall-and-protection-ddos-about]}

Special services are services that are not directly related to the core functionality of {var(cloud)} (virtual machines). However, when these services are used in combination with virtual machines, you can significantly expand the functionality of your cloud infrastructure — both in terms of protection and in terms of speed and flexibility.

## {heading(AntiDDoS)[id=firewall-and-protection-ddos-antiddos]}

DoS (Denial of Service) is a hacker attack on a computing system aimed at causing its failure, that is, creating conditions under which legitimate users of the system cannot access the provided system resources (servers), or this access becomes difficult. The failure of the attacked system can also be a step toward taking over the system (if in an abnormal situation the software outputs some critical information — for example, a version, a fragment of program code, etc.).

{var(cloud)} provides a DDoS attack protection service.

To enable the service or get a consultation about it, submit a request on the [AntiDDoS page](https://cloud.vk.ru/anti-ddos/).

## {heading(WAF)[id=firewall-and-protection-ddos-waf]}

Rules for filtering incoming and outgoing traffic to detect and block network attacks (Web Application Firewall).

The main difference from the AntiDDoS service is that the protection is software-based rather than hardware-based.

To enable the service or get a consultation about it, submit a request on the [WAF page](https://cloud.vk.ru/waf/).
