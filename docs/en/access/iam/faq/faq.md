{cut(How to leave a project?)}

In {var(cloud)} you cannot leave a project on your own. To leave a project, contact the project owner or administrator and ask them to remove your account from the list of participants.

If you cannot contact the project owner or they refuse to remove you, please contact [technical support](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru).

{/cut}

{cut(How can an owner or administrator remove a user from a project?)}

To remove a user from a project, use the {linkto(../instructions/access-manage#deleting_member)[text=instruction]}.

{/cut}

{cut(Is it possible to work under one account in different locations?)}

No. It is not possible to register accounts with the same email in different regions.

{/cut}

{cut(Can I verify a phone number with the same number used by the previous project owner?)}

No. One phone number can be linked to only one account.

{/cut}
