## {heading(Creating an event log resource)[id=logging-manage-resource-create]}

To identify logs of systems that are not part of PaaS, create a resource:

1. {ifdef(public)}[Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/){/ifdef}{ifndef(public)}{linkto(../../../../tools-for-using-services/account/instructions/lk-entry#tools-account-lk-entry)[text=Go to]}{/ifndef} the {var(cloud)} management console.
1. Go to the **Monitoring** → **Logging** section.
1. Click the **Settings** button.
1. Go to the **Other resources** tab and click the **Add resource** button.
1. Specify the resource name and click the **Add** button.

For information on binding objects to resources, see the section {linkto(../connect-plugin#logging-connect-plugin)[text=%text]}.

## {heading(Editing an event log resource)[id=logging-manage-resource-edit]}

1. {ifdef(public)}[Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/){/ifdef}{ifndef(public)}{linkto(../../../../tools-for-using-services/account/instructions/lk-entry#tools-account-lk-entry)[text=Go to]}{/ifndef} the {var(cloud)} management console.
1. Go to the **Monitoring** → **Logging** section.
1. Click the **Settings** button.
1. Go to the **Other resources** tab.
1. Click the **•••** icon to the right of the resource name and select **Edit**.
1. Change the resource name and click the **Save changes** button.

## {heading(Deleting an event resource)[id=logging-manage-resource-delete]}

1. {ifdef(public)}[Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/){/ifdef}{ifndef(public)}{linkto(../../../../tools-for-using-services/account/instructions/lk-entry#tools-account-lk-entry)[text=Go to]}{/ifndef} the {var(cloud)} management console.
1. Go to the **Monitoring** → **Logging** section.
1. Click the **Settings** button.
1. Go to the **Other resources** tab.
1. Click the **•••** icon to the right of the resource name and select **Delete**.
1. Confirm the deletion.
