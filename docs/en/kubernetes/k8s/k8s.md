The service has CNCF certification and is compatible with the standard Kubernetes API. Thanks to autoscaling, applications on Managed Containers get additional power instantly during peak loads and reduce consumption when loads drop. Autoscaling saves up to 60% on computation.

[First-generation](/en/kubernetes/k8s/concepts/cluster-generations) clusters use [Magnum](https://docs.openstack.org/magnum/latest/user/), an OpenStack tool for container orchestration, to automate their deployment and scaling.

{note:warn}
The functionality of clusters might vary depending on their generation. This section describes the capabilities of first-generation clusters only. Documentation for second-generation clusters is available in the [Managed Containers for second-generation clusters](/en/kubernetes/mk8s) section.

Creating first-generation clusters from the management console is disabled for new users and is only available on request via [technical support](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru).
{/note}
