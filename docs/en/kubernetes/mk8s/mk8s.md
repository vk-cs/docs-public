The service has CNCF certification and is compatible with the standard Kubernetes API. Thanks to autoscaling, applications on Managed Containers get additional power instantly during peak loads and reduce consumption when loads drop. Autoscaling saves up to 60% on computation.

[Second-generation](/en/kubernetes/mk8s/concepts/cluster-generations) (or managed) clusters are maintained and managed by the VK Cloud platform, minimizing the need for routine manual management by users.

{note:warn}
The functionality of clusters might vary depending on their generation. This section describes the capabilities of second-generation clusters only. Documentation for first-generation clusters is available in the [Managed Containers for first-generation clusters](/en/kubernetes/k8s) section.
{/note}
