# {heading(For partners)[id=start-about-partners]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

The {var(cloud)} partner program allows partner companies to earn revenue by reselling cloud services and offering their customers solutions based on {var(cloud)} infrastructure. Grow your own business with the help of cloud technologies and services and gain benefits by bringing new service consumers to the {var(cloud)} platform.

Depending on the selected {linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models)[text=cooperation model]}, the following participation options are available to partners:

- Partner (reseller partner) — implements {var(cloud)} cloud services for businesses. Can operate under the White Label concept — reselling cloud services under their own brand.
- Agent — provides {var(cloud)} customer acquisition services and earns a commission on resource or license consumption.

{note:info}

If you plan not only to resell cloud services but also to implement your own solutions based on the {var(cloud)} platform, see the {linkto(../partners-certification#partners-certification)[text=certification information]}.

{/note}

For attracted consumers or sales of cloud services, partners receive remuneration that depends on the selected {linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models)[text=cooperation model]} and {linkto(#start-about-partners-status)[text=partner status]}.

## {heading(Advantages of the partner program)[id=start-about-partners-advantages]}

The following benefits are available to {var(cloud)} partner program participants:

- Deal protection for your client.
- {linkto(#start-about-partners-study)[text=Training]} for you and your team on designing, implementing, and operating {var(cloud)} solutions.
- {linkto(#start-about-partners-study)[text=Training]} on selling cloud services and confirmation of seller competency.
- Joint events and PR campaigns. Ready-made marketing materials for market promotion.
- {linkto(#start-about-partners-status)[text=Status system]}. The higher the status, the higher the partner's remuneration.
- A {linkto(../../../tools-for-using-services/partner-platform#partner-platform)[text=platform]} for managing clients and their projects.
- Infrastructure for piloting solutions in the cloud. Partners can receive {var(cloud)} resources free of charge or at a discount to launch pilot projects and demonstrate solutions to potential customers.
- {linkto(../../support#start-support)[text=Technical support]} of {var(cloud)} for partners and their clients.
- Access to the {var(cloud)} service development roadmap.
- Special terms for VK Tech products.

## {heading(How to participate in the program)[id=start-about-partners-how-become]}

1. Select a {linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models)[text=cooperation model]}.
1. {linkto(../become-partner#start-become-partner)[text=Become a partner]}.
1. (Optional) Contact your partner relationship manager or write to [partner@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:partner@mcs.mail.ru) to complete {linkto(#start-about-partners-study)[text=training]} and confirm your competencies.
1. Attract clients and {linkto(#start-about-partners-deal-check-in)[text=register]} deals.
1. Receive remuneration.

## {heading(Training)[id=start-about-partners-study]}

Partners can participate in a professional training program that includes:

- Lectures, demos, and lab work from {var(cloud)} engineers and architects.
- Technical training with a focus on designing, implementing, and operating {var(cloud)}.
- Sales training. After training, the partner and their team can obtain competency certificates:
    - Sale,
    - Pre-Sale.

## {heading(Deal registration)[id=start-about-partners-deal-check-in]}

To become eligible for an increased commission and raise your partner status, {linkto(../../../tools-for-using-services/partner-platform/instructions/deals-and-applications/registration#pp-deal-registration)[text=register]} your deal with a client in the partner program system.

When registering a deal, the partner:

- secures the right to remuneration;
- receives special terms (discounts, bonuses, priority support);
- ensures protection from competition with other partners.

## {heading(Partner status)[id=start-about-partners-status]}

{linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models-partner)[text=Partners and White Label Partners]} obtain one of the statuses. The partner status depends on sales volumes, completed training, and other criteria. The partner status affects the amount of their remuneration.

Existing statuses:

- silver;
- gold;
- platinum.
