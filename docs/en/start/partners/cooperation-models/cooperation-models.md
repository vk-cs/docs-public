# {heading(Cooperation models)[id=partners-cooperation-models]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

The {var(cloud)} partner program offers two cooperation models:

- Partner,
- Agent.

## {heading(Partner)[id=partners-cooperation-models-partner]}

Sell {var(cloud)} cloud services to customers on your own and earn through the partner discount.

Any company (a legal entity or a sole proprietor) willing to promote and resell {var(cloud)} solutions can become a partner.

### {heading(How the model works)[id=partners-cooperation-models-partner-how]}

1. You attract a new customer.
1. You receive a {linkto(#partners-cooperation-models-partner-discount)[text=partner discount]} on {var(cloud)} solutions.
1. You sell the solution and earn through the price difference.

You handle the legal and financial aspects of customer relationships on your own.

When choosing this cooperation model, {var(cloud)} resources can be resold under your own brand following the White Label concept. White Label partners get access to unique functionality:

- Customization of the customer interface and documentation to match your brand.
- Management of customer plans and the prices they see in their management console.
- Changing the customer payment model, shortening the customer's path to start using {var(cloud)} services.

To become a White Label partner, contact the manager handling your partnership application, or write to [partner@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:partner@mcs.mail.ru).

### {heading(What the partner discount depends on)[id=partners-cooperation-models-partner-discount]}

The partner discount consists of two parts:

- The base discount is 10%.
- The deal registration discount is up to 20%.

The total discount can reach up to 30% of the price listed in the {var(cloud)} [price list](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist).

## {heading(Agent)[id=partners-cooperation-models-agent]}

Attract new customers to {var(cloud)} cloud services and receive a {linkto(#partners-cooperation-models-agent-reward)[text=reward]} for customer consumption.

An agent can be:

- A legal entity.
- A sole proprietor registered more than 1 year ago.

### {heading(How the model works)[id=partners-cooperation-models-agent-how]}

1. You recommend {var(cloud)} solutions to customers.
1. {var(cloud)} sells the solution to the customer.
1. You receive a reward for the customer's consumption of platform resources.

All legal and financial aspects of customer relationships are handled on the {var(cloud)} side.

### {heading(What the reward depends on)[id=partners-cooperation-models-agent-reward]}

The agent reward consists of two parts:

- The base reward is a percentage of {var(cloud)} service payments made by the customers you attract.
- The reward for conducting a technical presale.

Your reward is fixed.

## {heading(What does not qualify for a reward)[id=partners-cooperation-models-no-reward]}

Regardless of the chosen cooperation model, no reward is given for the following customer purchases:

-	Purchases made with bonus rubles on the balance.
-	Purchases of products from the Application Store.
-	Acquisition of licenses from third-party vendors.
-	Paid infrastructure administration services.
