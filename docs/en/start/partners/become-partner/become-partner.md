# {heading(How to become a partner)[id=start-become-partner]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

A {var(cloud)} partner is a legal entity that cooperates with the cloud service provider under the official terms of the partner program. The partner acts as an intermediary between the provider and end customers, participating in the promotion, sales, implementation, and support of cloud solutions.

1. Prepare a preliminary development plan with {var(cloud)} (pipeline): a list of potential deals that includes all current and planned projects from the initial contact to the completion of the deal.

   If you are ready to {linkto(../about-partners#start-about-partners-deal-check-in)[text=register a deal]} with your first potential customer, instead of the development plan, be prepared to {linkto(../../../tools-for-using-services/partner-platform/instructions/deals-and-applications/registration#pp-deal-registration)[text=submit a request]} for deal registration.

1. Fill out the application on the [website](https://cloud.vk.ru/partner), providing your company details. A {var(cloud)} partner manager will contact you and tell you about the next steps.
1. Discuss with the manager the {linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models)[text=cooperation model]} that suits you and the preliminary development plan with {var(cloud)} (pipeline).
1. Sign the partnership agreement.

Once you have signed the partnership agreement, you will get access to the {var(cloud)} {linkto(../../../tools-for-using-services/partner-platform#partner-platform)[text=partner platform]} — an interface for registering deals and managing customers and their projects.

{note:warn}
If you have not received a response to your application, write to [partner@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:partner@mcs.mail.ru).
{/note}
