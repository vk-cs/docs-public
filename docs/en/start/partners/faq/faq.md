# {heading(Cooperation models)[id=partners-faq]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

{cut(Who can become a partner program participant?)}

Legal entities and individual entrepreneurs who have executed and signed a reseller agreement with VK Tech can become partners of {var(cloud)}.

{/cut}

{cut(How to become a partner?)}

To become a partner of {var(cloud)}, fill out an application using the {linkto(../become-partner#start-become-partner)[text=instructions]}. A {var(cloud)} manager will contact you as soon as possible.

If your application has not received a response for a long time, send a request to [partner@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:partner@mcs.mail.ru).

{/cut}

{cut(Where can I learn more about the White Label cooperation model from {var(cloud)}?)}

General information about the model is provided in the {linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models-partner-how)[text=documentation]}, and specific details of cooperation can be clarified with the partner relations manager in the following ways:

- by submitting a partnership application or using the **Get a consultation** button on the [website](https://cloud.vk.ru/partner);
- by writing to [partner@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:partner@mcs.mail.ru).

{/cut}

{cut(How will payments between me and {var(cloud)} be made?)}

Every month you will receive reporting documents with a detailed breakdown of your customers' consumption. The invoice will come from the {var(cloud)} accounting department.

{/cut}

{cut(At what price can I sell {var(cloud)} resources?)}

It depends on the selected {linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models)[text=cooperation model]}.

A {linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models-partner)[text=Partner]} of {var(cloud)} can set any price for services for their customers. If necessary, {var(cloud)} can disable the display of their prices in your customer's {linkto(../../../tools-for-using-services/partner-platform/concepts/client-account#partner-platform-client-account)[text=management console]}. To do this, write to [partner@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:partner@mcs.mail.ru).

Customers who purchased {var(cloud)} services on the recommendation of an {linkto(../cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models-agent)[text=agent]} purchase them at the prices listed in the {var(cloud)} [price list](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist).

{/cut}

{cut(How to get a {var(cloud)} partner certificate?)}

To obtain a certificate, you need to complete competency-based {linkto(../about-partners/#start-about-partners-study)[text=training]}.

{/cut}

{cut(What competencies can be confirmed with a partner certificate?)}

{var(cloud)} partners have access to {linkto(../about-partners/#start-about-partners-study)[text=training]} in two areas:

- Sale;
- Pre-sale.

{/cut}

{cut(How to sign up for training?)}

To sign up for {linkto(../about-partners/#start-about-partners-study)[text=training]}, contact your partner relations manager.

{/cut}

{cut(Where can I find sales and marketing materials for partners?)}

Materials are available only to active partners after signing the partnership agreement.

{/cut}

{cut(How to get infrastructure for piloting projects?)}

Infrastructure can be obtained after signing the agreement. If you are already a partner of {var(cloud)}, report your infrastructure needs via the [support portal](https://support.mcs.mail.ru), by email [partner@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:partner@mcs.mail.ru), or through your partner relations manager.

{/cut}

{cut(What tools for managing customer projects does a {var(cloud)} partner get?)}

Partners get access to the {var(cloud)} {linkto(../../../tools-for-using-services/partner-platform#partner-platform)[text=partner platform]}, which allows them to invite customers, register deals, and manage customer projects.

{/cut}

{cut(Does {var(cloud)} provide support to partners' customers?)}

Yes, {var(cloud)} specialists provide a partner's customers with the same full 24/7 support as direct customers.

{/cut}

{cut(The balance of my customer's project account shows a debt, although the customer paid me for the services. Why?)}

The {var(cloud)} platform does not track your customers' payments, so these funds are not automatically credited to the customer account. If you want to credit funds to the customer's project balance, contact [technical support](/en/contacts).

In your email, specify the top-up amount and the project {linkto(../../../tools-for-using-services/partner-platform/instructions/client-management/client-information#pp-client-information-view-projects)[text=PID]}, after which the funds will be credited. You can top up the account either on a regular basis or with a one-time payment of any amount you specify.

{/cut}

{cut(What causes changes in customer charges?)}

Questions about calculations within the partner program require an individual analysis of account data. Contact your personal manager or [technical support](/en/contacts).
{/cut}

{cut(Did not find the answer to your question?)}

Write to [partner@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:partner@mcs.mail.ru)

{/cut}
