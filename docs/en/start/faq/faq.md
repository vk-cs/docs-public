{cut(How to contact support?)}

Use one of the communication channels listed in the {linkto(../support/support-info#support-info)[text=Support Terms]}.

{note:info}

To speed up request processing, provide technical support with your login, project name, and describe your issue in detail.

{/note}
If possible, attach any available diagnostic information: screenshots, logs, and other materials.
{/cut}

{cut(How fast does support respond?)}

Information about response and resolution times can be found in the {linkto(../support/sla#support-sla)[text=SLA]} section.

{/cut}

{cut(What rights are needed to contact technical support?)}

To create a technical support ticket, a user only needs to have any {linkto(../../access/iam/concepts/roles-reference#iam-roles-reference)[text=role]} in the project.

{/cut}

{cut(How do I find out my manager's contacts?)}

To connect new projects and negotiate cooperation terms, contact the [sales department](https://cloud.vk.ru/contacts/).

{/cut}

{cut(What are your working hours?)}

You can contact technical support at any time — 24/7.

{/cut}

{cut(Where are {var(cloud)} data centers located?)}

The {linkto(../concepts/architecture#architecture-az)[text=list]} of data centers and availability zones.

{/cut}

{cut(Where can I view the current SLA metrics of {var(cloud)}?)}

The {linkto(../support/sla#support-sla)[text=Service Level Agreement (SLA)]}. Actual availability metrics and incident reports are published on the platform status page.

{/cut}

{cut(How do I change my phone number?)}

To change the phone number linked to your account, contact [technical support](/en/contacts). The support team will contact the owner of the number to confirm the change.

{/cut}

{cut(I cannot register with my phone number)}

Registration via the {var(cloud)} management console is only possible for Russian numbers starting with +7.

{/cut}

{cut(How do I unlink my phone number from another account?)}

It is not possible to unlink a phone number from an account you have lost access to on your own. Contact [technical support](/en/contacts).

{/cut}

{cut(How do I change the email linked to my account?)}

To change the email linked to your account, contact [technical support](/en/contacts).

{/cut}

{cut(I forgot my account password)}

Recover your account password. To do this, click the **Recover password** button on the login screen.

{/cut}

{cut(I have registered, but the services in my management console are still unavailable)}

Services become available after all stages of {linkto(../../intro/onboarding/account/create-account#onboarding-create-account)[text=registration]} are completed. Check that your {linkto(../../intro/onboarding/account/create-account#onboarding-create-account-confirm-email)[text=email]} and {linkto(../../intro/onboarding/account/create-account#onboarding-create-account-confirm-phone)[text=phone]} have been verified.

{/cut}

{cut(What payment methods are available and how do I enable them?)}

The available {linkto(../../intro/billing/concepts/payment-methods#billing-payment-methods)[text=payment methods]} depend on the region and are selected when {linkto(../../intro/billing/instructions/payment#billing-payment-balance-charge)[text=adding funds]} to the project's balance.

You can set up {linkto(../../intro/billing/instructions/add-card#billing-add-card-refill)[text=automatic top-up]} of the project's balance using a linked bank card.

To make payments as a legal entity or individual entrepreneur, {linkto(../../intro/billing/instructions/corporate#billing-corporate)[text=reconfigure your project]}.

{/cut}

{cut(What is the minimum amount needed to activate an account?)}

To gain access to {var(cloud)} services, top up your project balance with any amount. It is recommended to deposit an amount equal to the daily consumption. To calculate the cost of the configuration you need, use the [calculator](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricing) or review the [price list](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist/).

{/cut}

{cut(How do I view my payment history?)}

To view all charges and payments across projects, go to the [balance page](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/services/billing) in your management console. For more details, see the {linkto(../../intro/billing/instructions/detail#billing-detail)[text=Expense Details]} article.

{/cut}

{cut(How do I change my pricing plan?)}

For individuals, it is not possible to change the pricing plan.

Legal entities and individual entrepreneurs can {linkto(../../intro/billing/instructions/corporate#billing-corporate-individual_contract)[text=conclude an individual contract]} with {var(cloud)}, which may include an individual pricing plan.

{/cut}

{cut(How do I enable two-factor authentication?)}

Follow the {linkto(../../access/iam/instructions/manage-2fa#iam-manage-2fa)[text=Enabling 2FA]} instructions.

{/cut}

{cut(I have lost my two-factor authentication app)}

Contact [technical support](/en/contacts). Specialists will verify that you are the account owner and then deactivate this option for your account. After that, enable two-factor authentication again in your [management console](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/account/profile).

{/cut}

{cut(What should I do if my account has been compromised?)}

Immediately try to {linkto(../../tools-for-using-services/vk-cloud-account/instructions/account-manage/editinfo#account-manage-editinfo-password)[text=change your password]}. If that fails, contact [technical support](/en/contacts) and in your ticket select the service type **VK Cloud** → **Report a problem** → **Personal account and user account** → **Account has been hacked**.

{/cut}

{cut(How do I manage roles and access permissions in a project?)}

Manage access permissions via the management console. Management is available only to the owner, superadministrator, and user administrator (IAM) {linkto(../../access/iam/concepts/rolesandpermissions#iam-concepts-rolesandpermissions)[text=roles]}. For more details, see the {linkto(../../access/iam/instructions/access-manage#iam-access-manage)[text=Access Management]} article.

{/cut}

{cut(I did not receive bonus points upon registration)}

Bonus points are granted when creating the first project in {var(cloud)}; subsequent projects are not eligible for this opportunity.

{/cut}

{cut(I do not have enough quotas)}

{linkto(../../tools-for-using-services/account/instructions/project-settings/manage#project-increase-quota)[text=Request]} a quota increase in your management console.

{/cut}

{cut(How do I delete my account?)}

Account deletion is performed upon a request to technical support made on behalf of the project owner's account. Before deleting your account, delete all existing resources and also unlink your bank card (if any).

{/cut}

{cut(I am not receiving emails from technical support)}

If you have not received a reply by email:

- Check the **Spam** and **Trash** folders in your mailbox.
- If you have your own mail domain, check whether the MX record is configured correctly on it.
- Make sure that other emails reach the selected email address. To do this, send a test email to it from any other mailbox.
- Contact support through another communication channel.

{/cut}

{cut(Can I register in {var(cloud)} without the status of an individual entrepreneur or legal entity?)}

Yes, {linkto(../../intro/onboarding/account/create-account#onboarding-create-account)[text=registration]} in {var(cloud)} is possible for individuals.

{/cut}

{cut(Where can I view service performance and outage data?)}

This data is published on the [system status page](https://status.msk.cloud.vk.ru).

{cut(Where do I change the legal entity details?)}

{/cut}

Also, review the {linkto(../support/support-info#support-info-individual)[text=full list of Professional Services]}.

{cut(Can I host and use VPN servers?)}

Hosting VPN servers for personal or corporate use is allowed. The user must comply with the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation, including the provisions of the Federal Law "On Communications" and other applicable regulations. Using VPN servers to provide access to information resources whose access is restricted within the Russian Federation is not permitted.

{/cut}

{cut(Where can I find the current public offer and a standard corporate contract template?)}

The current offer and other legal documents are available on the [official {var(cloud)} website](../legal/archive/digital-cloud). If you need to conclude a bilateral contract, contact the [sales department](https://cloud.vk.ru/contacts/).

{/cut}

To obtain a copy of the contract or other legal documents related to your project, [contact technical support](/en/contacts).

{cut(Are there certified and protected solutions (Federal Law-152)?)}

For hosting systems that require protection under Federal Law-152, we offer an accredited segment of the platform. For a consultation on these solutions, [contact the sales department](https://cloud.vk.ru/contacts/).

{/cut}

{cut(Can I set up backup of on-premises servers to the cloud?)}

To back up data from your on-premises server to the cloud, install a utility on it (for example, Veeam Agent) and configure scheduled delivery of backups to the {linkto(../../storage/s3#s3-main)[text=object storage]}.

{/cut}

{cut(Is there a dedicated server hosting service?)}

Yes, {var(cloud)} offers dedicated server (Bare Metal) rental services. For a consultation on these solutions, [contact the sales department](https://cloud.vk.ru/contacts/).

{/cut}

{cut(I have noticed suspicious activity related to my resources. What should I do?)}

To file a complaint about unauthorized use of resources, including network scanning, attacks, or illegal proxying, write to [abuse@cloud.vk.com](mailto:abuse@cloud.vk.com) or [contact technical support](/en/contacts), selecting the ticket subject "Complaint/Abuse".

{/cut}

If you have not found an answer to your question on the portal, [contact technical support](/en/contacts).
