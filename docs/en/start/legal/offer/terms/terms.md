## Terms and Definitions

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===

| Term
| Definition 

| **Project Administrator**
| A person with the necessary legal capacity and capacity to act, who is the Project Owner or a Project User to whom the Project Owner has granted the right to manage the Project.

| **Bucket**
| Disk space with Content placed on it.

| **Customer's Bonus Balance (Account)**
| A set of records in the Personal Account reflecting the accrual/write-off by the Company of points (bonuses) to the Customer during promotional campaigns, when paying compensation to the Customer, or in other cases established by the Company. The Bonus Balance is reduced by the amount of Services connected by the Customer.

| **Project Owner**
| A Customer who created the Project using the Platform's functionality. The Project Owner has full rights to manage the Project, including the ability to grant Project Administrator rights to another person, change the scope of granted rights, revoke such person's Project Administrator rights, create and delete User Accounts for Users within the Project, as well as grant and restrict such Users' access to selected Services.

| **Impact**
| A Ticket attribute that records the degree to which an event affects the quality of provision of one or more Services.

| **Reaction Time**
| The period of time from the moment the User sends information to Technical Support until the moment the User is sent confirmation of the Ticket registration in the form of an email with registration details (Ticket number, main attributes).

| **Resolution Time**
| The period of time from the moment the Ticket is registered until the moment the Ticket is assigned the "Completed" status and the User is sent information about the results of the work performed.

| **Inbound Traffic**
| The amount of information from the "Internet" passing through the virtual computing capacities and other cloud resources of the Platform provided to the Customer.

| **Documentation (User Documentation)**
| Information posted at the link: [https://cloud.vk.com/docs/](https://cloud.vk.com/docs/), as well as other information posted on the Website and (or) in the Personal Account containing instructions on connecting and using the Services and other functionality of the Products and Services.

| **Service Availability**
| A Service quality control parameter defined as the percentage ratio of the time during which the Service performs its declared functions to the time during which the Service functionality is unavailable. The target value of the parameter and its calculation method are specified in the SLA.

| **Customer**
| A legally capable individual or legal entity that is a party to the Agreement on the use of the Platform Services.

| **Change Request (CR)**
| A Ticket that registers the need for changes to the Services.

| **Information Request (Consultation)**
| A Ticket in which the User requests specific information, instructions, or a consultation related to the operation of the Product or Services. A Consultation is a special case of a Service Request.

| **Service Request (SR)**
| A User's Ticket that involves an action (performance of work) that is standard in terms of information technology, related to an event that is part of the normal operation of an IT service and cannot lead to a service outage or a decrease in its quality.

| **Change**
| The addition, modification, or removal of anything that could affect the composition or quality of provision of the Products and Services.

| **Incident**
| Any unplanned event that has caused or may cause a loss of Product functionality, or an interruption or degradation of the quality of provision of one or more Services; a Ticket Type for which specific processing parameters specified in the SLA apply.

| **Outbound Traffic**
| The amount of information sent to external information systems, passing through the virtual computing capacities and other cloud resources of the Platform provided to the Customer, which is billed under various Services in the manner and on the terms specified on the Website.

| **Technical Support Team (Technical Support Specialist)**
| A team of specialists (a Specialist) with the necessary competencies to ensure the operation of specific Services, Products, information resources, systems, or infrastructure configuration elements.

| **Company**
| Limited Liability Company "Digital Cloud" (OGRN 1197746546568, 125167, address of location: Moscow, inner-city territory of the Khoroshyovsky Municipal District, Leningradsky Prospekt, bldg. 39, bldg. 80, room 1/9), which provides Customers with services for granting access to the Platform and Services.

| **Compensation**
| The amount of bonus points credited by the Company to the Customer's Bonus Balance in case of violation of the Service quality requirements specified in the SLA. Compensation does not involve payment of monetary funds to the Customer.

| **Content**
| Documents, graphic images, photographs, scripts, texts, videos, music, sounds, messages, and other objects (files) in digital form placed by the Customer or Users on the Platform resources when using the Service.

| **Customer's Personal Account (Main Balance)**
| A set of records in the Personal Account reflecting the financial relationship between the Customer and the Company regarding the provided Services. The Main Balance is increased by the amount of funds transferred by the Customer and credited by the Company to the Customer's Personal Account for prepayment of Services, and is reduced by the cost of the Services connected by the Customer.

| **Personal Account**
| A closed area of the Platform that allows an authorized User to manage settings, edit the Account, access statistical data, and perform other actions necessary for the implementation of the Platform's functionality and individual Services.

| **Service Unavailability Period**
| The time interval from the moment the Customer's Ticket about the inability to use the Service functionality is registered with the Company's TSS, which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, until the moment the Customer is notified of the completion of recovery operations.

| **Zero Balance**
| The absence (zero or negative value) of bonuses/monetary funds on the Customer's Bonus and Main Balance (Personal Account) for the Company to write off funds to pay for the Services.

| **Ticket**
| An information object of the TRS, a card in which the fact of interaction with an authorized User of the Products and Services and further processing of this event are recorded.

| **Workaround**
| A method of resolving an Incident that allows the restoration of Service provision or functionality in the shortest possible time without eliminating the cause of the Incident, without waiting for the implementation of a full-fledged permanent solution.

| **Reporting Period**
| The period of time during which the Customer is provided with the Services. Unless otherwise specified in the relevant agreements on the use of individual Services, the Reporting Period is one calendar month.

| **Partner**
| A person with whom the Company has concluded an agreement in order to enable Customers to use the Partner's products and/or services through the Platform

| **Platform**
| A combination of infrastructure components, software, and competencies of the Company's specialists that defines a set of Services, access to which is granted to the Customer under the Agreement. For the purposes of this document, the Platform means the VK Cloud Platform accessible to the Customer through the Website.

| **Project User (User)**
| A person with the necessary legal capacity and capacity to act, for whom the Project Administrator has created an Account and who uses the Service functionality. A Project User is a user of the Services to which the Project Administrator has granted them access.

| **Pricelist**
| Information posted at https://cloud.vk.com/pricelist specifying the Tariffs for Services within the VK Cloud Platform.

| **Priority**
| A Ticket attribute that determines the target completion time for work, according to the SLA, and the order in which Tickets are taken into work by the Technical Support Specialist.

| **Project**
| A logically connected set of web pages that are associated with a specific domain name or other identifier and operate according to the Platform's algorithms. Within a single Project, using the Platform's web interface, Accounts are created for the Project Users, who may be granted access to one or more Platform Services.

| **Incident Resolution**
| Actions that make it possible to restore the provision of the Service or the Product functionality.

| **Website**
| The Company's information resource located at [https://cloud.vk.com](https://cloud.vk.com/), providing the Customer and Users with access to the Platform and Services. The Company posts information on the Website that is binding on the Customer and Users.

| **Tariff Plan/Tariff**
| The pricing terms for the provision of Services, defined in the Pricelist.

| **Technical Support**
| A comprehensive service for assisting Users in operating the Products and Services, providing consulting, performing standard requests, and diagnosing and resolving incidents that arise.

| **Service (Product)**
| A functional component implemented as part of the Platform that ensures the performance of specific tasks and the achievement of specific results and is used in the Customer's business processes.

| **Account**
| A set of data (login, password, additional factors) used for identification and authentication of the Customer or Project User to grant access to the Services

| **Self-Service Portal (Portal)**
| The TRS interface available to authorized representatives of the Customer. It is used for registering and tracking the status of Tickets, interacting with Technical Support, and accessing other Portal functions described in the User Documentation.

| **Target Time**
| A parameter limiting the maximum time within which the work on a Ticket must be completed, and the description of the solution or work performed must be recorded in the Ticket card and sent to the User. Target values of the parameter depending on the type and priority of the Ticket are specified in the SLA.

| **SLA (Service Level Agreement)**
| An agreement between the Company and the Customer describing the measurable parameters of Service provision and their target values. The SLA is an integral part of the Agreement.
