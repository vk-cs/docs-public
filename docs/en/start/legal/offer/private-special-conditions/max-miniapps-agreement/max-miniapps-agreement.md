## 4. Composition of the Service

4.1. The comprehensive Service includes the following components:

4.1.1. 1 (one) dedicated public IP address;

4.1.2. Unlimited traffic within the dedicated public IP address;

4.1.2. Traffic protection service (WAF) for traffic passing through the dedicated IP address. The cost of the Service includes traffic scrubbing of up to 100 Mbit/s at the moment rules are triggered.

The L7 (WAF) protection system does not restrict the entire bandwidth 24/7. The WAF uses a set of rules and activates filtering/bandwidth restriction only upon detection of violations or attacks on legitimate traffic.

The totality of the listed components constitutes one unit of the Service for billing purposes.
