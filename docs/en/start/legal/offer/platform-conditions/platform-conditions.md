## 1. General Provisions

1.1.	The Company provides the Customer with access to the Platform, which allows the Customer to use the Personal Account to gain access to the Platform's Services.

1.3.	To use the Platform, the Customer must register on the Platform and create an Account. When creating an Account on the Platform, the Customer enters their login (their email address) and password twice in a special window with input fields, and also confirms their agreement with the Applicable Rules. To confirm registration, an email is sent to the email address specified by the Customer, containing a link to confirm the creation of an Account on the Platform.

1.4.	Authorization and login to the Platform are performed through the Website. A person who has logged in to the Platform using the Customer's login and password is deemed the proper owner of the corresponding account, access to the use and management of which was obtained as a result of such authorization.

1.5.	After authorization on the Platform (login to the Personal Account), the Customer may use the Platform and Services provided that these Conditions and the Applicable Rules are observed.

1.6.	Connection, configuration, and management of the Services are performed by the Customer using the Personal Account tools. The use of a Service begins from the moment the Customer is granted remote access to the corresponding Service.

1.7.	The Platform allows the Customer to perform the following actions:

* configure their Account within the functional capabilities of the Platform;
* create and delete Projects using the functional capabilities of the Platform;
* connect Services available through the Platform, including to created Projects, and also discontinue the use of connected Services;
* create accounts for individuals with the necessary legal capacity and capacity to act (Users) within Projects, and also delete created accounts;
* grant persons for whom accounts have been created access to the Platform's Services, restrict or close access to these Services;
* grant Project Administrator rights to another person, change the scope of granted rights, up to and including revocation of the right to manage the Project.
* perform other actions related to the use of the Platform and Services that are not prohibited by the legislation of the Russian Federation and the Applicable Rules.
