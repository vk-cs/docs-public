This document (hereinafter — the "Agreement") defines the Service Level of the Dedicated Servers service / VK Bare Metal (hereinafter — the "Service") and is an integral part of the Agreement and the General Service Level Agreement posted on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/offer/service-level-agreement/common-agreement](/ru/start/legal/offer/service-level-agreement/common-agreement).

## 1. Service Availability Percentage

1.1. The Service Availability Percentage for the Reporting Period is calculated using the following formula:

(Maximum Service availability time – Service Downtime) / Maximum Service availability time × 100%

1.2. The Maximum Service availability time is the maximum possible number of minutes of Service operation during the Reporting Period, taking into account the restrictions specified in the General Service Level Agreement.

1.3. The guaranteed Availability level for a single dedicated server is **99.9%** during the Reporting Period.

## 2. Conditions for Recognizing a Server as Unavailable

2.1. A single dedicated server is considered unavailable if network connectivity with the server is lost from the Internet or an internal network, as recorded by the Company's monitoring tools or confirmed by the Customer through a request to the support service.

2.2. This Agreement does not apply to cases of server Availability loss due to circumstances beyond the Company's control, including external malicious impact on the server or actions of the Customer that led to server Unavailability.

## 3. Amount of Compensation for Unavailability

3.1. In case of violation of the guaranteed Availability level, Compensation is provided in the form of bonuses to the Customer's Bonus account, in an amount depending on the actual Availability percentage for the Reporting Period:

| Service availability percentage | Compensation amount |
| --- | --- |
| 99.90% and above | not provided |
| less than 99.90% – 99.0% and above | 10% |
| less than 99.00% – 95.0% and above | 20% |
| less than 95.0% | 25% |

3.2. The amount of Compensation is calculated as a percentage of the cost of all dedicated servers for the Reporting Period for which Unavailability was recorded in the corresponding Reporting Period.

3.3. The Compensation is credited in the form of bonus points, which are not convertible into monetary funds.

3.4. The Customer agrees that the Compensation provided for in this Agreement is an exhaustive measure of the Company's liability to the Customer for Service Unavailability. The Customer acknowledges that the conditions for payment of Compensation provided for in this Agreement are proportionate and fair consequences of the violation, and also exclude the possibility of applying other forms of liability to the Company for Service Unavailability.

## 4. Compensation for Hardware Component Failure

4.1. In case of server component failure, the Company undertakes to replace them with analogous ones at its own expense within **24 (twenty-four) hours** from the moment the Customer contacts the support service.

4.2. If the repair is not completed within 24 (twenty-four) hours from the moment of failure, starting from the next hour the Dedicated server is considered unavailable, and its downtime is included in the Availability calculation.

4.3. If fully identical components are not available, components with analogous or improved characteristics may be installed without increasing the cost of the Service for the Customer.
