This document defines the level of provision of the VK Cloud Platform Services (hereinafter — the "Agreement") and is an integral part of the Agreement concluded between Digital Cloud LLC (hereinafter — the "Company") and the Customer.

## 1. Subject of the Agreement

1.1.	This Agreement defines the guaranteed quality parameters of the VK Cloud Platform Services provision, target values of the parameters, as well as the terms of Compensation in case the target values of the Service quality parameters are not achieved.

1.2.	This Agreement includes general terms of Services provision and Private Service Level Agreements specific to individual Services (hereinafter — "Private SLAs"), which are appendices to this Agreement. In case of contradictions between the terms of this Agreement and the Private SLAs, the terms of the relevant Private SLAs shall prevail.

1.3.	A mandatory condition for the Company to provide guarantees regarding the level of Services provision is the conclusion of an Agreement by the Customer (including by acceptance of the Offer), as well as acceptance and compliance with the requirements of the documents referenced in the Agreement.

## 2. Availability

2.1.	Availability is a key parameter of Services provision, which is defined as the percentage ratio of the time during which the Service performs its declared functions to the time during which the Service functionality is unavailable.

2.2.	The target availability value for each individual Service is determined individually in the Private SLAs.

2.3.	Availability of Services is calculated using the formula:
(AT – UT) / AT x 100%
AT — availability time of the Services;
UT — unavailability time of the Services based on registered Customer Requests and/or the Company's own data.

2.4.	To calculate Service Availability, the time period from the moment a Request containing information about Service Unavailability is registered until the moment the Customer is sent a notification about the completion of restoration works is used. If the Company has additional data about the beginning of Service unavailability, the Company may take this data into account in its calculations.

2.5.	The reporting period for calculating Services Availability is 1 (one) calendar month. If Service Unavailability begins in one Reporting period and ends in the next one, the Service Unavailability is fully attributed to the Reporting period in which the greater part of the Unavailability occurred.

2.6.	The Customer is obliged to assist the Company in identifying the causes of failures and in restoring the operability of the Services.

## 3. Exceptions

3.1.	The Agreement does not apply to periods of Unavailability caused by the following circumstances:

* actions of the Customer and/or third parties, including but not limited to the use of third-party equipment and/or software, resulting in Service Unavailability;
* violation by the Customer of technical restrictions on the use of Services and/or the use of Services in excess of the established Quotas and Limits;
* Service Unavailability arose as a result of Maintenance or Emergency recovery works;
* violation by the Customer of the terms of the Agreement, Applicable rules, Documentation;
* Services Unavailability arose due to circumstances of force majeure;
* Services Unavailability arose in compliance with the requirement of state or municipal authorities or a judicial act on the suspension of Services provision;
* during the processing of a Request, it was established that Services Unavailability was caused by direct actions (inaction) of the Customer or third parties, including but not limited to: changes to settings that directly or indirectly affect software or equipment located in the Company's area of responsibility, made without agreement with the Company.

3.2.	The Company reserves the right to carry out Maintenance works with full or partial Service Unavailability. Unavailability related to Maintenance works is not subject to Compensation in the manner prescribed by the Private SLAs, provided that the Customer is notified at least 24 (twenty-four) hours before the start of such works.

3.3.	The Company reserves the right to carry out Emergency recovery works with Service interruption without prior notification of the Customer.

3.4.	Unavailability related to Emergency recovery or Maintenance works is not subject to Compensation in the manner prescribed by the Private SLAs, if the total Service Unavailability time does not exceed 8 hours per Reporting period.

3.5.	Official channels for notification of scheduled Maintenance works are:

* the notification menu in the Customer's Personal account;
* the service status page ([https://status.msk.cloud.vk.com](https://status.msk.cloud.vk.com));
* email distribution.

4.	Procedure for obtaining Compensation

4.1.	In case the Company fails to comply with the guaranteed Service Level, the Customer has the right to receive Compensation upon request (if provided for by the Private SLA for the relevant Service). To receive Compensation, the Customer must send a request by email (qualitycontrol@tech.vk.com) no later than 10 (ten) business days from the end of the Reporting period in which Service Unavailability was recorded. The request must contain:

* the Customer's account identifier (email address);
* the project identifier;
* dates, time and duration of Service unavailability;
* description of the request;
* the number of the support service request registered at the moment of recording the Unavailability.

4.2.	Within 10 (ten) business days from the date of receipt of the request, the Company undertakes to provide the Customer with Compensation or a reasoned refusal to provide Compensation.

4.3.	The amount of Compensation depends on the actual Service Availability and the total cost of the consumed Service in the Reporting period in which Unavailability was recorded. The amount of Compensation is determined individually for each Service.

4.4.	When calculating the Service Availability Percentage for a Reporting period, the Company's data is used. If the Company has its own data on the beginning of Unavailability indicating an earlier start time of Unavailability than the time the Request was submitted by the Customer, the Company may use such data.

4.5.	In case of loss and/or damage to the Customer's data as a result of an Incident caused by the fault of the Company, the Customer is provided with Compensation, based on the Customer's Request, in the amount of 100% of the cost of the consumed Service affected by the Incident for the Reporting period during which the data loss or damage occurred.

4.6.	The Company may refuse the Customer Compensation in case the Customer has overdue debt for Services payment until it is repaid.

4.7.	The Company may refuse the Customer Compensation in case of missing the deadline for submitting a request established by clause 4.1 of this Agreement.

4.8.	Compensation is credited in the form of bonuses that are credited to the Customer's Bonus account and can be used exclusively to pay for the Company's Services. Cash payment of Compensation is not provided.

4.9.	The maximum amount of Compensation may not exceed the amount of the Service actually consumed by the Customer in the Reporting period in which Service Unavailability was recorded.

4.10.	Compensation is the sole and exclusive remedy for the Customer for non-compliance with the guaranteed level of service. The Company is not liable for indirect losses of the Customer, including lost profits or damage to business reputation related to Services unavailability.
