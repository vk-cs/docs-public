This Research Participation Agreement (the "Agreement") is posted on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/feedback-form/research-agreement]() (the "link") and is available on the form page at the link [https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/](https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/) and is concluded only with those persons to whom the said link has been previously provided on behalf of Digital Cloud LLC.

Whereas:

- Digital Cloud LLC (the "Organizer") is a legal entity whose main activity is aimed at developing, implementing, and promoting Internet services and products (including computer programs and mobile applications).
- The Organizer is interested in conducting objective research and testing products and services to assess their attractiveness and ergonomics for Internet users, analyze consumer properties, and use the research (testing) results in order to improve products and services, define the Organizer's marketing strategy, conduct advertising campaigns, and for scientific, informational, educational, and other similar purposes for which the research (testing) is conducted.

The Organizer invites individuals (the "Respondent") to voluntarily participate in research (testing) of the Organizer's products and services.

## 1. Subject and Other Terms

1.1. The Respondent undertakes to participate in the Organizer's research (testing) under the terms set forth in this Agreement, and the Organizer undertakes to accept the research (testing) results from the Respondent.

1.2. The Respondent undertakes to:

- provide truthful and necessary information for the purposes of a specific research (testing);
- answer the Organizer's questions provided as part of the research (testing);
- perform actions necessary for the purposes of conducting the research (testing);
- complete the tasks that constitute the research (testing).

1.3. The Respondent's research (testing) may take place both online and with the Respondent's direct participation in the research (testing) at the Organizer's office.

1.4. The Organizer has the right to use the information received from the Respondent in its activities for the analysis and development of products and services, for conducting advertising/marketing campaigns, as well as for publication on the Internet and use in any public statements of the Organizer for informational, scientific, educational, and other similar purposes during the term of this Agreement; to disclose and use the results of the analysis of the information received from the Respondent in research results, analytical materials and reports, marketing research, advertising materials, and other similar materials in any form. At the same time, the Organizer undertakes not to use the information received from the Respondent in ways that damage his/her honor, dignity, or business reputation.

1.5. The Organizer undertakes to process the data received from the Respondent in accordance with the procedure defined by this Agreement and the legislation of the Russian Federation.

1.6. During the research (testing), the Respondent undertakes to take measures ensuring the security of his/her personal information or information of third parties that has become known to the Respondent, and to prevent third parties from accessing it. The Respondent bears sole responsibility for the disclosure of such information during the research (testing). At the same time, if during the research the Organizer accidentally becomes aware of the Respondent's personal information or the personal information of third parties for whom the Respondent has not taken security measures, the Organizer may warn the Respondent of the need to comply with security measures. At the same time, the Organizer has no intention of using the said information.

1.7. The Respondent gives the Organizer consent to the processing of his/her personal data, namely: full name, contact information (phone number, email), and other information necessary for the conclusion and execution of this Agreement with the Respondent, both with and without the use of automation tools, in the following ways: collection, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification, use, transfer (dissemination, provision, access), blocking, depersonalization, destruction. The purpose of processing the Respondent's personal data is the conclusion and fulfillment of obligations under this Agreement.

1.8. The Respondent understands and agrees that during the research (testing) the Organizer may record the Respondent's behavioral activity, which is due to the need to conduct the research (testing) and obtain objective research (testing) results.

1.9. If the Respondent is a minor, this Agreement is concluded with his/her legal representative, and the legal representative must fill out the form at [https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/](https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/) with his/her own details and the Respondent's details.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1. The Parties have specifically stipulated that any information belonging to the Organizer and having actual or potential value for the Organizer due to its being unknown to third parties and the absence of free lawful access to it, which the Respondent may access during participation in the research (testing), is confidential. Confidential information includes, without limitation:

a. any information obtained as part of participation in the research (testing) of the Organizer's products and services, including new ones;

b. technical information, including information about the Organizer's information systems, methodological and regulatory materials on the use and operation of information systems, research and testing reports, projects, drawings, information about the materials used, production methods, object and source code of computer programs, as well as visualized images of the above information;

c. business information, including the Organizer's archival data, pricing procedures, sales strategy, marketing plans, and research data;

d. strategic information, including proposals for new products, services, or markets; plans or negotiations on the acquisition and disposal of property;

e. information of third parties provided to the Organizer under confidentiality terms: information about business activities, technologies, methods, software, as well as other non-public data of the Organizer's counterparties, as well as affiliates and business partners of the Organizer;

f. other information that may have actual or potential value for the Organizer; information that i) has been designated as confidential by the Organizer, or ii) the circumstances of whose provision allow the conclusion that it is confidential, or iii) whose nature allows the conclusion that it is confidential.

2.2. The Respondent undertakes to comply with and take all measures within his/her power to protect the Organizer's confidential information and not to disclose it to third parties without the Organizer's consent.

2.3. Confidential Information always remains the property of the Organizer and must not be transferred to any third parties, copied, or otherwise reproduced or used in any way without the prior written consent of the transferring Party.

2.4. The obligation to keep Confidential Information secret in accordance with the terms of this Agreement comes into force from the moment the Agreement is signed by both Parties and remains in force for 10 (ten) years after the expiration of this Agreement or its early termination.

2.5. In the event of a disclosure of confidential information, the Respondent undertakes to compensate the Organizer for all losses caused as a result of such disclosure.

## 3. Other Terms

3.1. This Agreement is concluded by filling out the form posted at the link [https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/](https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/) and clicking the "Submit" button located below the form. After performing these actions, the completed form will be automatically sent to the email address [uxlab@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:uxlab@mcs.mail.ru).

3.2. This Agreement is valid for 10 (ten) years from the moment the Respondent concludes the Agreement.

3.3. In cases of non-performance and/or improper performance of obligations under the Agreement, the Parties bear liability in accordance with the current legislation of the Russian Federation.

3.4. The Agreement may be terminated by agreement of the Parties or at the request of the Respondent by sending an appropriate notice to the address of the Organizer's location.
