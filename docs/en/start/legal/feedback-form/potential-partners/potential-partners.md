This Agreement defines the terms of information exchange between the Company and the Applicant.
The Applicant unconditionally and fully accepts the terms of the Agreement by submitting a completed questionnaire for potential partners of the Company containing a link to this Agreement (hereinafter — the "Questionnaire") via a web form on one of the Company's websites (in accordance with Part 3 of Article 438 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation).

## 1\. Subject of the agreement

1.1. The Company provides the Applicant with the following services free of charge (hereinafter — the "Services"):

  I. Reviewing the Applicant for compliance with the requirements set for the Company's partners in order to make a decision on concluding, maintaining, amending, and terminating contracts by the Company.

  II. Recording the Applicant as a potential partner of the Company, including simplifying repeated reviews of the Applicant for compliance with the requirements set for the Company's counterparties.

1.2. For the purpose of providing the Services, the Company may receive from the Applicant and process information about the Applicant and their representatives, including:

* Full name of the Applicant — an individual/ sole proprietor;
* Full name of the Applicant's representative;
* Position of the Applicant's representative;
* Applicant's email;
* Applicant's phone number;
* Email of the Applicant's representative;
* Phone number of the Applicant's representative;
* Name of the Applicant — a legal entity;
* Applicant's website;
* Advertising identifiers.

1.3. The Applicant guarantees that the data specified in the Questionnaire was provided by them personally, is truthful, correct, and valid, and agrees to allow the Company to verify the provided data.

1.4. The Applicant hereby gives the Company, for the duration of the Agreement, consent to the processing (collection, receipt, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, transfer (provision, access), use, blocking, deletion, destruction) of the personal data provided during the completion of the Questionnaire, using automation tools or without them, for the purposes of fulfilling this Agreement for the duration of the Agreement.

1.5. For the purpose of fulfilling the Agreement (receiving the Services), the Applicant instructs the Company, for the duration of the Agreement, to process information about the Applicant's representatives and contact persons as provided for in this Agreement, using automation tools or without them, by means of: receipt, recording, systematization, accumulation, provision of access, storage, clarification (updating, modification), use, blocking, deletion, destruction.

The Applicant guarantees the existence of legal grounds for the Company to process, upon the Applicant's instruction, information about the Applicant's representative and contact persons specified in the Table. If such legal grounds cease to apply to the respective data subject, the Applicant undertakes to immediately notify the Company by letter to the Company's address of location, indicating the web form through which the Questionnaire was submitted to the Company.

The Company undertakes to ensure the confidentiality of information processed upon the Applicant's instruction within the framework of this Agreement. The Company undertakes to comply with the requirements of applicable legislation regarding the protection of information processed upon the Applicant's instruction.

The Applicant guarantees that they have notified the Applicant's representatives and contact persons about the processing of their data by the Company upon the Applicant's instruction under the terms of this Agreement.

1.6. The Applicant hereby consents to the transfer by the Company of personal data listed in clause 1.2 of this Agreement to third parties with whom the Company has concluded the relevant contract (hereinafter — the "Contractors"), exclusively for the purposes specified in clause 1.2 of the Agreement, in particular, to assist the Company in processing Questionnaires, providing feedback to Applicants on Questionnaires, and conducting negotiations on concluding contracts for the use of the Company's services.

1.7. The Company undertakes to process the personal data received for the performance of this Agreement in the manner determined by the Agreement and the legislation of the Russian Federation.

1.8. If the legal grounds for processing information about the Applicant's representative cease to be valid for the Company, the Applicant must send the Company a written notification to the Company's address of location, indicating the web form through which the Questionnaire was submitted to the Company, as well as the details of the respective representative.

1.9. The Company has the right to store the personal data provided by the Applicant in the customer relationship management information systems owned by the Company.

## 2\. Term, amendment, and termination of the agreement

2.1. This Agreement comes into force for the Applicant from the moment it is accepted by the Applicant and remains in effect until terminated by agreement of the Parties, or at the request of one of the Parties on the grounds and in the manner provided for by the Agreement and the current legislation of the Russian Federation.

2.2. The Applicant may terminate this Agreement by sending the Company a notification by letter to the Company's address of location, indicating the web form through which the Questionnaire was submitted to the Company. This Agreement is deemed terminated after 30 calendar days from the date the Company receives the Applicant's notification.

2.3. The Company may continue processing the personal data provided by the Applicant after the Applicant sends a notification of termination of the Agreement if there are other legal grounds for their processing, which may include the need to fulfill other agreements to which the Applicant is a party, as well as the need to perform the functions, powers, and obligations imposed on the Company by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

2.4. This Agreement may be amended by the Company provided that the Applicant is notified by posting a new version of the specified documents on the Website. The Applicant undertakes to independently and periodically check the Agreement for changes. Amendments to the Agreement made by the Company come into force on the day following the day of their publication on the Website.

2.5. In the event of non-performance and/or improper performance of obligations under the Agreement, the Parties bear liability in accordance with the current legislation of the Russian Federation.

## 3\. Dispute resolution

3.1. All disputes related to the conclusion, interpretation, performance, and termination of the Agreement shall be resolved by the Parties through negotiations. Compliance with the pre-trial claims procedure for dispute resolution is mandatory. Claims must be reviewed by the Party that received the claim within 30 (thirty) calendar days from the date of its receipt.

3.2. If it is impossible to resolve disagreements through negotiations, the Parties may apply to judicial authorities for dispute resolution. In such a case, the dispute shall be referred to the appropriate court at the Company's location, unless otherwise provided by the legislation of the Russian Federation.
