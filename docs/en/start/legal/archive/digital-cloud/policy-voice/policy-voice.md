## 1\. Terms and definitions

**Website** — an Internet website located at [https://cloud.vk.ru](https://cloud.vk.ru) that provides the Customer with access to the Service. The Company posts information on the Website that is mandatory for the Customer.

**VK Cloud Platform (Platform)** — a computer program operated by the Company and accessible to the Customer through the Website, which provides the Customer with access to the selected Services.

**Speech Recognition and Synthesis Service (hereinafter the Service)** — a service of the Company known under the name "Cloud Voice", which consists in providing the Customer with access to an API through which requests with tasks are sent and service results are received. Cloud Voice generates speech based on the received task in the form of text, as well as recognizes speech from audio files and streaming audio. The Service operates using machine learning models.

**Service Unavailability (Downtime)** — a time interval from the moment the Customer sends a message to the Company's support service by email at support@mcs.mail.ru or via the feedback form in the Personal Account, the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, until the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes restoration works.

**Personal Account** — a closed area of the Platform that allows the Customer to manage settings, edit the Customer's Account, access statistical data, as well as perform other actions necessary for the implementation of the functionality of the Platform and individual Services.

**Personal Account Balance (Ledger Account)** — a set of records reflecting the financial relationship between the Customer and the Company regarding the services provided. The Customer's balance increases by the amount of funds transferred by the Customer to the Company and credited by the Company to the Ledger Account as prepayment.
