**Reporting Period** — a calendar month.

**Applicable Rules** — this Agreement, available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-1cgrm](/ru/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-1cgrm), containing the terms of access to and use of the Service, as well as the following documents, to the extent necessary for using the Service:

**Personal Account** — a closed area of the Platform that allows the Customer to manage settings, edit the Customer's Account, access statistical data, and perform other actions necessary for the functioning of the Platform and individual Services.

The Customer's balance increases by the amount of funds transferred by the Customer to the Company and credited by the Company to the Personal Account Balance as advance payment for services, and decreases by the cost of the services connected by the Customer.
