This document constitutes an official offer of Digital Cloud LLC (hereinafter — the "Company") to conclude a user agreement (hereinafter — the "Agreement") with you (hereinafter — the "Customer") on the provision of services for granting access to the Infra virtual infrastructure Service within the "VK Cloud" Platform (hereinafter — the "Platform"), on the terms set forth in this Agreement, which is available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-infra](/en/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-infra).

## 1\. Terms and Definitions

**Website** — the website located at [https://cloud.vk.ru](https://cloud.vk.ru), which provides the Customer with access to the Service. The Company posts information on the Website that is mandatory for the Customer.

**"VK Cloud" Platform** (**Platform**) — a computer program operated by the Company and available to the Customer through the Website, which provides the Customer with access to selected Services.

**Virtual Infrastructure Service (Service)** — a service of the Company known under the name "Virtual Infrastructure Service" (Infra), which consists in providing the Customer with the ability to create virtual servers and virtual disks on the Company's servers.

**Content** — electronic documents, graphic images, photographs, scripts, texts, video, music, sounds, computer programs, and other objects (files) uploaded by the Customer and hosted on the Service.

**Prohibited Content** — any Content described in clause 2.6 of this Agreement, as well as other materials the use of which is prohibited by the Applicable Rules.

**Service Unavailability (Downtime)** — the time interval between the moment the Customer sends a message to the Company's support service by e-mail at [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru) or via the feedback form in the management console, the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, and the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes recovery work.

**Compensation** — the amount of bonus points to be credited to the Customer's Bonus balance in the event of Downtime in the manner prescribed by this Agreement. Compensation does not involve the payment of monetary funds to the Customer.

**Billing Period** — a calendar month.

**Applicable Rules** — the rules for using the Company's Service, available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-infra](/en/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-infra), containing the terms of access to and use of the Service and set forth in the following documents:

- Terms of Use of the "VK Cloud" Platform ([https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/legal-terms](/en/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/legal-terms));
- Company's Privacy Policy ([https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-privacy](/en/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-privacy)).

If this Agreement uses terms for which definitions are not given in Section 1 of this Agreement, the definitions given to such terms in the Applicable Rules shall apply.

6.4. The Company guarantees that the services for providing access to the Service are provided 24 (twenty-four) hours a day, 7 (seven) days a week.

6.5. The Company reserves the right to interrupt the operation of the Service for necessary maintenance work, including on business days. Such cases will not be considered interruptions in the provision of services if the Company notifies the Customer 24 (twenty-four) hours in advance of the start of maintenance work that will cause interruptions in the operation of the Service, by publishing the relevant information on the Website (including the duration of the maintenance work). Service unavailability time associated with maintenance work is not subject to compensation under the rules of clause 6.6 of the Agreement.

6.6. Service unavailability ("Downtime") is defined as the time interval between the moment the Customer sends a message to the Company's support service via the feedback form available in the Personal Account, containing a notification of Service unavailability, or a message by e-mail at: [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru), the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, and the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes recovery work. If the Company has its own data on the start of the Downtime indicating an earlier start time of the Service unavailability than the time of sending the message by e-mail to [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru) or via the feedback form in the management console, the Company may use such data. Disputes about the start and end time of the Downtime are resolved through negotiations between the Parties. If the Downtime began in one Billing Period and ended in the next Billing Period, the Service unavailability is entirely attributed to the Billing Period in which the majority of the Downtime occurred.

6.7. The amount of Compensation depends on the actual uptime of the Service in question and its cost charged from the Personal balance in the Billing Period in which the Downtime was recorded.

For the purposes of calculating the Compensation, the cost of other services available on the Platform for which no Downtime was recorded is not taken into account.

In the event of Service unavailability, the Company undertakes, at the Customer's request, to transfer the Compensation to the Customer (credit it to the Bonus balance (account)), the amount of which is determined on the basis of the table below (hereinafter — Table No. 1):

| Uptime (in %)\* | Downtime (min or h) in one Billing Period in total | Compensation amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 99.95% and above | less than 22 minutes | not paid |
| 99.9%–99.95% | from 22 to 45 minutes | 3% of the amount charged from the Personal balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 99.0%–99.9% | from 45 minutes to 7.5 hours | 10% of the amount charged from the Personal balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 95.0%–99.0% | from 7.5 hours to 36 hours | 20% of the amount charged from the Personal balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 90.0%–95.0% | from 36 hours to 73 hours | 25% of the amount charged from the Personal balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| less than 90% | more than 73 hours | 50% of the amount charged from the Personal balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |

_\*The calculation is based on 43,800 minutes in a Billing Period_

6.8. In any cases of violation of the Service level guarantees, the Customer undertakes to cooperate with the Company to identify the source of the problems, eliminate it, and restore the operation of the Service or remote/damaged Content.

6.9. Within 10 (ten) business days from the date of receipt of the compensation request regarding the Service unavailability, the Contractor undertakes to provide a response to the compensation request, which will specify the conditions for providing the Customer with compensation for the Service unavailability or a reasoned refusal to provide it.

6.10. The amount of Compensation during a Billing Period on both grounds specified in clauses 6.6 and 6.7 of the Agreement may not exceed the amount paid by the Customer for the Billing Period in which the events that served as the basis for the Compensation occurred.

6.11. Service unavailability caused by the actions of the Customer or third parties, or by force majeure circumstances that resulted in the unavailability of services, is not subject to Compensation; nor is the suspension of services associated with scheduled maintenance work, as defined in clause 6.5 of this Agreement; nor is the suspension of services at the request of state or municipal bodies in cases and in the manner determined by applicable law. Under no circumstances does the Company compensate the Customer of the Service for indirect losses, including lost profit and damage to business reputation.
