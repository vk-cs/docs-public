For the purposes of calculating the Compensation, the cost of other services available on the Platform for which no Downtime was recorded is not taken into account.

The amount of Compensation is determined on the basis of the table below (hereinafter — Table No. 1):

| Uptime (in %)\* | Downtime (min or h) in one Billing Period in total | Compensation amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 99.95% and above | less than 22 minutes | not paid |
| 99.9%–99.95% | from 22 to 45 minutes | 3% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 99.0%–99.9% | from 45 minutes to 7.5 hours | 10% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 95.0%–99.0% | from 7.5 hours to 36 hours | 20% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 90.0%–95.0% | from 36 hours to 73 hours | 25% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| less than 90% | more than 73 hours | 50% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Billing Period in which the Downtime occurred |

\* *The calculation is based on 43,800 minutes in a Billing Period*
