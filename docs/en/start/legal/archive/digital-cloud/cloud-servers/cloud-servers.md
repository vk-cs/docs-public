## 1. Terms and Definitions

**Website** — the internet website located at [https://cloud.vk.ru/](https://cloud.vk.ru), which provides the Customer with access to the Service. The Company publishes on the Website information that is binding on the Customer.

**"VK Cloud" Platform (Platform)** — a computer program operated by the Company and available to the Customer via the Website, which provides the Customer with access to the selected Services.

**Virtual Infrastructure Service (Service)** — a service of the Company known as the "Virtual Infrastructure Service" (Cloud Servers), which consists in enabling the Customer to create virtual servers and virtual disks on the Company's servers.

**Content** — electronic documents, graphic images, photographs, scripts, texts, videos, music, sounds, computer programs, and other objects (files) uploaded by the Customer and hosted on the Service.

**Prohibited Content** — any Content described in Section 2.6 of this Agreement, as well as other materials whose use is prohibited by the Applicable Rules.

**Service Unavailability (Downtime)** — the time interval between the moment the Customer sends a message to the Company's support service by email to [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru) or via the feedback form in the management console, the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, and the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes restoration works.

**Compensation** — an amount of bonus points to be credited to the Customer's Bonus Balance in the event of Downtime in accordance with the procedure provided for in this Agreement. Compensation does not involve any payment of money to the Customer.

**Reporting Period** — a calendar month.

**Applicable Rules** — the rules for using the Company's Service, available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/cloud-servers](/en/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/cloud-servers), which contain the terms of access to and use of the Service and are set forth in the following documents:

- Terms of Use of the "VK Cloud" Platform ([https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/legal-terms](/en/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/legal-terms));
- Company's Privacy Policy ([https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-privacy](/en/start/legal/archive/digital-cloud/policy-privacy)).

If this Agreement uses terms for which no definitions are given in Section 1 of this Agreement, the definitions of such terms from the Applicable Rules shall apply.

If the Downtime began in one Reporting Period and ended in the next Reporting Period, the Service unavailability is attributed entirely to the Reporting Period in which the greater part of the Downtime occurred.

6.7. In the event of Service unavailability, the Company undertakes, at the Customer's request, to transfer to the Customer (credit to the Bonus Balance (account)) the Compensation, the amount of which depends on the actual uptime of the Service in question and its cost debited from the Personal Balance in the Reporting Period in which the Downtime was recorded.

For the purposes of calculating the Compensation, the cost of other services available on the Platform for which no Downtime was recorded is not taken into account.

The amount of Compensation is determined on the basis of the table below (hereinafter — Table No. 1):

| Availability time (in %)\* | Unavailability time (min or h) in one Reporting Period in total | Compensation amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 99.95% and above | less than 22 minutes | not paid |
| 99.9%–99.95% | from 22 to 45 minutes | 3% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 99.0%–99.9% | from 45 minutes to 7.5 hours | 10% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 95.0%–99.0% | from 7.5 hours to 36 hours | 20% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 90.0%–95.0% | from 36 hours to 73 hours | 25% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| less than 90% | more than 73 hours | 50% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |

_\*The calculation is based on 43,800 minutes in a Reporting Period_
