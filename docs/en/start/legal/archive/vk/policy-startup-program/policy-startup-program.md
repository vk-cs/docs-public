The Startup Support Program from VK Cloud (hereinafter referred to as the “**Program**”) has been developed by LLC “VK” (PSRN: 1027739850962) (hereinafter referred to as the “**Organizer**”) and is intended to attract and retain on the “VK Cloud” Platform companies (legal entities or individual entrepreneurs) developing digital products and services.

For Participants who registered an Account on the VK Cloud Platform on February 1, 2025 or later, the Rules of the startup support program hosted on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-startup-program](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-startup-program) shall apply.

## 1. Terms and Definitions

**Program** — the startup support program from VK Cloud, providing special terms of use of the VK Cloud, VK WorkSpace and VK Ads platforms (jointly — the **Platforms**) for Participants of the Program.

**Rules** — this document, which is a public offer, hosted on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-startup-program](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-startup-program).

**Program Term** — the term of the Program during which applications for participation in the Program may be submitted.

**VK Cloud Platform** / **VK Cloud** — a computer program available to the User via the website [https://cloud.vk.ru/](https://cloud.vk.ru/) and operated by the Organizer, which allows the User, in accordance with the terms of the Agreement, to create and delete Projects, create and delete Accounts for Project Users within such Projects, as well as grant access to and restrict access of such Users to selected Services.

**VK WorkSpace Platform** / **VK WorkSpace** — a computer program available to the user via the website [https://workspace.vk.ru](https://workspace.vk.ru), which allows the user, in accordance with the terms of the agreement on the VK WorkSpace Platform, to create and delete projects, create and delete accounts for end users of projects within such projects, as well as grant access to and restrict access of such end users to selected VK WorkSpace services.

**VK Ads Platform** / **VK Ads** — a software and hardware complex created for the purpose of providing advertising placement services, located at [https://ads.vk.ru/](https://ads.vk.ru/), including providing the advertiser with the ability to independently place advertising materials, monitor and keep statistical records of placed advertising materials.

**Agreement** — an agreement on the provision of access services to the VK Cloud Platform, concluded between the Organizer and the User, including the Terms of Use of the VK Cloud Platform, Agreements on the use of individual Platform Services, the Organizer's Privacy Policy, hosted on the website [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/vk](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk).

**User** — a person possessing the necessary legal capacity to conclude the Agreement, who has created an Account on the VK Cloud Platform. A User who has been granted the status of a Program Participant by the Organizer in accordance with the conditions listed in Section 3 of the Rules is also referred to as a “**Program Participant**”.

**Account** — information provided by the User (the availability of which is a necessary condition) for registration and subsequent authorization of the User on the VK Cloud Platform in the process of the Organizer providing services to them, which consists of a name (login), a password for accessing the Personal Account (password), as well as other information. Access to the Account is carried out through the Personal Account.

**User Personal Account** — a closed area of the VK Cloud Platform that allows the User to manage settings, edit the User Account, access statistical data, as well as perform other actions necessary for the implementation of the functional capabilities of the VK Cloud Platform and individual Services.

**Price List** — the web page [https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist) indicating the cost of the Services of the VK Cloud Platform.

**Service** — a software module of the VK Cloud Platform that makes it possible to implement individual functional capabilities within the VK Cloud Platform, which are provided by the Organizer or the Organizer's partners. Granting the User the right to use any Service does not depend on granting access to other Services.

**Customer Bonus Balance (account)** — a set of records reflecting the accrual/write-off by the Organizer of points to the User during promotional campaigns or when paying the User compensation in connection with the Organizer's violation of the agreed service level. The Bonus Balance is reduced by the amount of services connected by the User on the VK Cloud Platform (including when applying the special terms of use of VK Cloud established by Section 5.1 of these Rules). If there are funds in both the Bonus and Personal Balance, write-offs are made first from the Bonus Balance, and then from the Personal Balance.

**Customer Personal Balance (account)** — a set of records reflecting the financial relationship between the User and the Organizer regarding the services provided. The User's Balance increases by the amount of funds transferred by the User to the Organizer and credited by the Organizer to the User's balance as prepayment for services, and decreases by the cost of services connected by the User.

If the Rules use terms for which definitions are not given in this Section of the Rules, the definitions of these terms from the Agreement and (or) the agreements on the use of the VK WorkSpace and VK Ads Platforms accepted by the User on the websites of the respective Platforms are used.

## 2. Program Term

2.1. The Program is valid from August 7, 2023 to December 31, 2026 inclusive. During this period, the User has the right to submit an application for participation in the Program.

The special terms of use of the Platforms provided for in Section 5 of the Rules are valid for 6 (six) months from the date the User is granted the status of Program Participant.

## 3. Conditions of Participation in the Program

3.1. You can participate in the Program if you simultaneously:

- Are a new User of the VK Cloud services, which means: (1) if the User joined the Program (submitted an application for participation in the Program) in 2023: the User previously did not have an Account on VK Cloud, registered an Account on the VK Cloud Platform no earlier than “January 01”, 2023 and no later than “December 31”, 2023 and did not pay for Access Services to the Services in 2023; (2) if the User joins the Program in 2024: the User previously did not have an Account on VK Cloud, registered an Account on the VK Cloud Platform no earlier than “January 01”, 2024 and no later than “December 31”, 2024 and did not pay for Access Services to the Services in 2024; (3) if the User joins the Program in 2025: the User previously did not have an Account on VK Cloud, registered an Account on the VK Cloud Platform no earlier than “January 01”, 2025 and no later than “December 31”, 2025 and did not pay for Access Services to the Services in 2025.. At the same time, the Program Participant's account on the VK Cloud Platform must be registered as a legal entity or individual entrepreneur.
- Are developing a digital product or service (a product/service at the MVP stage or higher) and are its rights holder (the exclusive right to the product belongs to you).
- Have submitted an application for participation in the Program and received confirmation of participation from the Program Organizer.

3.2. To apply the special terms of use of the VK Ads and VK WorkSpace services listed in clauses 5.2, 5.3 of the Rules, the Program Participant must be a new user of the VK Ads and VK WorkSpace services (you are a new user if: (1) in case of joining the Program in 2023: you registered an account/management console on these Platforms no earlier than “January 01”, 2023 and no later than “December 31”, 2023 and did not pay for the services in 2023; (2) in case of joining the Program in 2024: you registered an account/management console on these Platforms no earlier than “January 01”, 2024 and no later than “December 31”, 2024 and did not pay for the services in 2024; (3) in case of joining the Program in 2025: you registered an account/management console on these Platforms no earlier than “January 01”, 2025 and no later than “December 31”, 2025 and did not pay for the services in 2025.. The Program Participant on the VK Ads Platform must be registered as a direct advertiser, and on the VK WorkSpace Platform, the Program Participant's account must be registered as a legal entity or individual entrepreneur.

3.3. **Restrictions**. Integrators, digital agencies implementing projects for third-party customer companies, and developers of products and services by order of companies that are their rights holders cannot join the Program.

## 4. Application for Participation in the Program

4.1. To participate in the Program, you must submit an application on the Website [https://cloud.vk.ru/startup](https://cloud.vk.ru/startup). Applications can be submitted until December 31, 2026 inclusive.

4.2. **By leaving an application for participation in the Program and clicking the “Submit application” button, the User, acting of their own free will and in their own interest, expresses their full and unconditional consent to the Organizer with the Program Rules**.

4.3. The application is reviewed by the Organizer within 5 (five) business days. Based on the results of reviewing the application and evaluating the User's compliance with the Program criteria specified in Section 3 of these Rules, the Organizer decides to grant the User the status of Program Participant or to refuse to grant such status. The User unconditionally agrees that the Organizer reserves the right to refuse the User the status of Program Participant at its own discretion without explanation.

Information about the decision made is sent to the User's email address specified when submitting the application.

## 5. Special Terms of Use of the Platforms for Program Participants

5.1. **The Program Participant is granted the following special terms of use of the VK Cloud Platform**:

5.1.1. Within 6 (six) months from the date of approval of the Application for participation in the Program, the Program Participant gets access to the Services specified in clause 5.1.2 on the VK Cloud Platform on special terms:

- 4 (four) months of using the Services at a 50% discount from the prices specified in the Price List
- 2 (two) months of a free trial period of using the Services.

The Participant has the right to independently determine the months of trial use and application of the 50% discount. The Participant must timely notify the accompanying manager from the Organizer of the choice of the month for trial use or the month in which the Participant wishes to apply the 50% discount.

The reporting period of using the Services for the purposes of acceptance and payment is 1 (one) calendar month. If the Participant joins the Program within a calendar month, the first incomplete reporting period (from the date of granting the Participant status and the start of using the Services until the end of the current calendar month) and the last incomplete reporting period (from the beginning of the calendar month until the expiration of the 6-month term of the special terms) are considered one full month for the purposes of applying the special terms.

**Restrictions on applying special terms**:

- The special terms apply to the following VK Cloud Services: Cloud Servers, VK Object Storage, Managed Containers, Cloud Databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, ClickHouse, Tarantool), Cloud Spark, Cloud Trino, Cloud Kafka, Cloud Flink, Cloud ML Platform, Vision, Voice, Cloud Backup.

  Payment for the use of other Services is made by the User on general terms based on the prices specified in the Price List.

- The trial period cannot be applied during the first month of participation in the Program.
- The trial period cannot be applied for 2 (two) consecutive months.
- The possibility of trial use of the Services is provided by crediting bonus points to the Participant's Bonus Account for the consumption of Services in the amount agreed with the accompanying manager of the Organizer. After the expiration of the calendar month, bonus points not used by the Participant during the trial month expire. Bonus points can be used exclusively to pay for the above Services and cannot be withdrawn by the Participant in monetary equivalent and (or) transferred to a third party.
- The maximum amount of benefits used by the Participant, including the amount of the 50% discount and the amounts of Bonus points credited to the Participant within the trial period, for the entire period of the Participant's participation in the Program is **no more than 2,000,000 (two million rubles), including VAT**. If this amount is exceeded, payment for the use of other Services is made by the User on general terms based on the prices specified in the Price List.

5.1.2. Consultation on the use of cloud services for hosting the User's product on the VK Cloud Platform (one consultation from a VK Cloud expert).

5.1.3. Free basic technical support in accordance with the terms posted on the website [https://cloud.vk.ru/](https://cloud.vk.ru/).

## 5.2. VK Workspace Platform

Within 6 (six) months from the date of approval of the Application for participation in the Program, the Program Participant is granted the right to use VK WorkSpace services at a 50% discount from the base cost of the “Basic” plan (the plan description is available at the link [https://workspace.vk.ru/tariffs/](https://workspace.vk.ru/tariffs/)). The subscription includes the following set of VK WorkSpace tools: business email, calendar, messenger with video calls, drive, and document editor.
After the expiration of 6 (six) months from the date of granting the Participant status, the User selects a plan for using VK Workspace services available on the website [https://workspace.vk.ru/tariffs/](https://workspace.vk.ru/tariffs/) and pays for it in full on standard terms.

## 5.3. VK Ads Platform

A VK Ads promo code is a special set of characters (letters and/or digits and/or other signs), the activation of which ensures the receipt (accrual) of a discount after topping up the balance of the Program Participant's personal (advertising) account with funds on the VK Ads service [https://ads.vk.ru/](https://ads.vk.ru/) (hereinafter — the **Promo Code**).

The Program Participant is provided with two Promo Codes:

1. The face value of the first Promo Code depends on the amount of the single payment that will be credited to the Participant's management console balance after entering the Promo Code in a special field:

   - when topping up the Participant's management console balance with a single payment in the amount of 5,000 (five thousand) rubles (excluding VAT) or more than 5,000 (five thousand) rubles (excluding VAT), the Promo Code face value is 5,000 (five thousand) bonus units (1 bonus unit = 1 ruble (excluding VAT));
   - when topping up the Participant's management console balance with a single payment in the amount of less than 5,000 (five thousand) rubles (excluding VAT), the Promo Code face value in the form of bonus units (1 bonus unit = 1 ruble (excluding VAT)) will be equal to the amount of the payment made.

1. The face value of the second Promo Code depends on the amount of the single payment that will be credited to the Participant's management console balance after entering the Promo Code in a special field:

   - when topping up the Participant's management console balance with a single payment in the amount of 10,000 (ten thousand) rubles (excluding VAT) or more than 10,000 (ten thousand) rubles (excluding VAT), the Promo Code face value is 10,000 (ten thousand) bonus units (1 bonus unit = 1 ruble (excluding VAT));
   - when topping up the Participant's management console balance with a single payment in the amount of less than 10,000 (ten thousand) rubles (excluding VAT), the Promo Code face value in the form of bonus units (1 bonus unit = 1 ruble (excluding VAT)) will be equal to the amount of the payment made.

Promo Code activation occurs in the Participant's management console by entering the Promo Code in a special field and subsequently topping up the Participant's management console balance with funds.

The Program Participant has the right to activate the received Promo Codes no more than 1 (once) within 3 (three) months from the moment of their receipt. Promo Codes are annulled if they are not activated by the Program Participant within the specified period.

The funds provided under the Promo Codes can be spent by the Participant only to pay for services of placing advertising materials in VK Ads. The Participant has no right to:

- withdraw the monetary equivalent of the discount amount (Promo Code) from the management console;
- transfer the right to receive the discount (Promo Code) to another Participant/third party, as well as transfer the discount amount (Promo Code) to the balance of another advertising account.

The Participant can receive Promo Codes only after 1 (one) calendar month of using VK Cloud services, that is, from the second month of participation in the Program.

Without using VK Cloud services for at least 1 month — the Promo Codes will be unavailable to the Program Participant.
The validity period of each Promo Code is 3 (three) months from the moment it is provided.

After the expiration of 6 (six) months from the date the Participant joined the Program, the User loses the right to apply the special terms of use of the Platforms listed in clauses 5.1-5.3 of the Agreement, regardless of their actual use.

## 6. Exclusion from the Program

The User may be deprived of the status of Program Participant in the event of any of the following circumstances:

- The User did not start using the special terms on VK Cloud within the first 2 (two) months from the moment of joining the Program. In this case, the Organizer notifies the User of the termination of the Participant status by sending a notification to the Participant's email address specified when submitting the application, 14 (fourteen) calendar days before the date of exclusion from the Program.
- Suspicious activity has been detected in the User's Project, or the Project does not comply with the Conditions of Participation in the Program. In this case, the Organizer notifies the User of the termination of the Participant status by sending a notification to the Participant's email address specified when submitting the application, 7 (seven) calendar days before the date of exclusion from the Program.

From the moment of the User's exclusion from the Program, the special terms of use of VK Cloud, VK WorkSpace and VK Ads provided for in Section 5 of these Rules cease to apply. Starting from the day following the day of the User's exclusion from the Program, payment for services on the VK Cloud, VK Workspace Platforms is made on the basis of the tariffs published on the websites of the Platforms. VK Ads Promo Codes, both activated and not activated at the moment of the User's exclusion from the Program, are annulled.
