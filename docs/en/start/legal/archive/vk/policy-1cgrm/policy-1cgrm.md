## 1. Terms and Definitions

**Website** — the website located at [https://cloud.vk.ru](https://cloud.vk.ru), which provides the Customer with access to the Service. The Company publishes information on the Website that is binding on the Customer.

**"VK Cloud" Platform (Platform)** — a computer program available to the Customer through the Website and operated by the Company, which provides the Customer with access to the selected Services.

**1C: Ready Workplace Service (Service)** — the Company's service known as "1C: Ready Workplace," which consists in providing the Customer with the ability to remotely access selected "1C:Enterprise 8" programs deployed in a cloud infrastructure via a browser or the 1C thin client operating through a browser.

**Content** — electronic documents, graphic images, photographs, scripts, texts, video, music, sounds, computer programs, and other objects (files) uploaded by the Customer and hosted on the Service.

**Service Unavailability (Downtime)** — the time interval between the moment the Customer sends a message to the Company's support service by e-mail at [support.1c.grm@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support.1c.grm@mcs.mail.ru) or via the feedback form in the Personal Account, the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, and the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes recovery work.

**Compensation** — an amount of bonus points credited to the Customer's Bonus Balance in the event of Downtime in the manner stipulated by this Agreement. The Compensation does not involve payment of funds to the Customer.

**Reporting Period** — a calendar month.

**Applicable Rules** — this Agreement, available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-1cgrm](/en/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-1cgrm), which contains the terms of access to and use of the Service, as well as the following documents to the extent necessary for using the Service:

If this Agreement uses terms for which definitions are not given in Section 1 of this Agreement, the definitions of such terms given in the Applicable Rules shall apply.

**Personal Account** — a closed area of the Platform that allows the Customer to manage settings, edit the Customer's Account, access statistics data, and perform other actions necessary for the operation of the Platform's functionality and individual Services.

**Customer Account** — a set of records reflecting the financial relationship between the Customer and the Company regarding the services provided. The Customer's Balance increases by the amount of funds transferred by the Customer to the Company and credited by the Company to the Customer Account as advance payment for services, and decreases by the cost of the services connected by the Customer.

**Module** — an application type of the 1C: Ready Workplace Service.
