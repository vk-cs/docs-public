## 1\. Terms and definitions

**Website** — an internet website located at [https://cloud.vk.ru/](https://cloud.vk.ru/), providing the Customer with access to the Service. The Company posts on the Website information that is mandatory for the Customer.

**«VK Cloud» Platform (Platform)** — a computer program operated by the Company and accessible to the Customer via the Website, which provides the Customer with access to selected services, including the Cloud Desktop Service.

**Virtual workspace** — a remote work computer located on the Company's server.

**Components of the virtual workspace** — software, including operating systems, purchased and installed by the Customer independently or, in cases expressly provided for, provided by the Company.

**Content** — electronic documents, graphic images, photographs, scripts, texts, video, music, sounds, computer programs and other objects (files) uploaded by the Customer or the Customer's end user via the Service.

**Compensation** — an amount of bonus points to be credited to the Customer's Bonus balance in case of Downtime in the manner prescribed by this Agreement. The Compensation does not imply payment of monetary funds to the Customer.

| Availability time (in %)\* | Unavailability time (min or h) in one Reporting Period in total | Compensation amount |

| less than 90% | more than 73 hours | 50% of the amount debited from the Personal Balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |

\* *The calculation is based on 43,800 minutes in the Reporting Period*
