This document is an official offer of LLC "VK" (hereinafter referred to as the "Company") to conclude a user agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") with you (hereinafter referred to as the "Customer") on the provision of services of granting access to the Cloud Servers virtual infrastructure Service within the "VK Cloud" Platform (hereinafter referred to as the "Platform"), on the terms set out in this Agreement, which is available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/cloud-servers](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/cloud-servers).

## 1. Terms and Definitions

**Website** — the internet website located at https://cloud.vk.ru/, which provides the Customer with access to the Service. The Company publishes on the Website information that is binding on the Customer.

**"VK Cloud" Platform (Platform)** — a computer program operated by the Company and available to the Customer through the Website, which provides the Customer with access to the selected Services.

**Virtual Infrastructure Service (Service)** — a service of the Company known as the "Virtual Infrastructure Service" (Cloud Servers), consisting in providing the Customer with the ability to create virtual servers and virtual disks on the Company's servers.

**Content** — electronic documents, graphic images, photographs, scripts, texts, videos, music, sounds, computer programs and other objects (files) uploaded by the Customer and posted on the Service.

**Prohibited Content** — any Content described in clause 2.7. of this Agreement, as well as other materials whose use is prohibited by the Applicable Rules.

**Service Unavailability (Downtime)** — the time interval between the moment the Customer sends a message to the Company's support service by e-mail to [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru) or via the feedback form in the management console, the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, and the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes restoration work.

**Compensation** — an amount of bonus points to be credited to the Customer's Bonus balance in the event of Downtime in the manner prescribed by this Agreement. The Compensation does not imply any payment of monetary funds to the Customer.

**Reporting Period** — a calendar month.

**Applicable Rules** — the rules for using the Company's Service, available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/cloud-servers](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/cloud-servers), containing the terms of access to and use of the Service and set out in the following documents:

- Terms of Use of the "VK Cloud" Platform ([https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/vk/legal-terms](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/legal-terms));
- Company's Privacy Policy ([https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-privacy/](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-privacy)).

If this Agreement uses terms for which definitions are not given in Section 1 of this Agreement, the definitions given to these terms in the Applicable Rules shall apply.

6.5. The Company reserves the right to interrupt the operation of the Service to carry out the necessary preventive maintenance, including on working days. Such cases will not be considered interruptions in the provision of services if the Company notifies the Customer 24 (twenty-four) hours in advance of the start of preventive maintenance causing interruptions in the operation of the Service, by publishing the relevant information on the Website (including the duration of the preventive maintenance). Service unavailability time related to preventive maintenance is not subject to compensation under the rules of clause 6.6. of the Agreement.

6.6. Service unavailability ("Downtime") is defined as the time interval between the moment the Customer sends a message to the Company's support service via the feedback form available in the Personal Account, containing a notification of the Service unavailability, or a message by e-mail to the address: [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru), the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, and the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes restoration work. If the Company has its own data on the start of the Downtime indicating an earlier start time of the Service unavailability than the time of sending the message by e-mail to [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru) or via the feedback form in the management console, the Company may use such data. Disputes about the start and end time of the Downtime are resolved through negotiations between the Parties. If the Downtime started in one Reporting Period and ended in the next Reporting Period, the Service unavailability is fully attributed to the Reporting Period in which most of the Downtime occurred.

6.7. In the event of Service unavailability, the Company undertakes, at the Customer's request, to transfer to the Customer (credit to the Bonus balance (account)) the Compensation, the amount of which depends on the actual availability time of the Service in question and its cost debited from the Personal balance in the Reporting Period in which the Downtime was recorded.

For the purposes of calculating the Compensation, the cost of other services available on the Platform for which no Downtime was recorded is not taken into account.

The amount of the Compensation is determined on the basis of the table below (hereinafter referred to as Table No. 1):

| Availability time (in %)\* | Unavailability time (min or h) in one Reporting Period in total | Compensation amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 99.95% and above | less than 22 minutes | not paid |
| 99.9%–99.95% | from 22 to 45 minutes | 3% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 99.0%–99.9% | from 45 minutes to 7.5 hours | 10% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 95.0%–99.0% | from 7.5 hours to 36 hours | 20% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 90.0%–95.0% | from 36 hours to 73 hours | 25% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| less than 90% | more than 73 hours | 50% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |

_\*The calculation is based on 43,800 minutes in a Reporting Period_

6.10. The amount of the Compensation during a Reporting Period on both grounds specified in clauses 6.6. and 6.7. of the Agreement cannot exceed the amount of funds paid by the Customer for the Reporting Period in which the events that served as the basis for the Compensation occurred.

6.11. The Service unavailability caused by the actions of the Customer or third parties, or by force majeure circumstances that resulted in the unavailability of services is not subject to Compensation; the suspension of the provision of services related to scheduled preventive maintenance, as defined in clause 6.5. of this Agreement; the suspension of the provision of services at the request of state or municipal bodies in the cases and manner determined by applicable law.
