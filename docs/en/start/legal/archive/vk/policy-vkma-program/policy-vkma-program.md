The VK Mini Apps Services Developer Support Program (hereinafter — the "**Program**") has been developed by Limited Liability Company "VK" (PSRN: 1027739850962) (hereinafter — the "**Company**") and is intended to attract and retain on the "VK Cloud" Platform companies (legal entities or individual entrepreneurs who are residents of the Russian Federation) and individuals developing mini-applications on the VK Mini Apps Platform.

The Partner of the Program is Limited Liability Company "V Kontakte" (PSRN: 1079847035179) (hereinafter — the "**Partner**"). The Partner is the owner of the VK Mini Apps Platform.

## 1. Terms and Definitions

**Program** — the VK Cloud program for supporting developers of VK Mini Apps services, providing for the accrual of bonus points to Program Participants on the grounds established in the Rules.

**Rules** — this document, which is a public offer, posted on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-vkma-program](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-vkma-program)

**Program Term** — the period of validity of the Program during which participation in the Program is possible and the Rules are in effect, as well as the accrual and use of Bonus Points is possible.

**Website** — an Internet website located at https://cloud.vk.ru/, providing the User with access to the VK Cloud Platform and various services. The Company posts on the Website information that is mandatory for Users.

**Agreement** — an agreement on the provision of services of access to the VK Cloud Platform, concluded between the Company and the Customer.

**Services** — services of access to the VK Cloud Platform and the Services selected by the Customer.

**VK Cloud Platform** — a computer program accessible to the Customer through the Website and operated by the Company, which allows the Customer, in accordance with the terms of the Agreement, to create and delete Projects, to create and delete within such Projects Accounts for Users of the Projects, as well as to grant and restrict such Users' access to the selected Services.

**VKontakte** — a social network known under the name "VKontakte", hosted on a website on the Internet at: [vk.ru](https://vk.ru) (including all levels of the specified domain, both those functioning as of the date of adoption of these Rules and those launched and put into operation during its entire term) and accessible to the User through the website, the mobile version of the website, applications and other resources, representing the result of intellectual activity in the form of a computer program, which, among other things, is a unique VKontakte database. The social network is presented in an objective form as a set of data and commands, and the audiovisual displays generated by them (including graphic images and the user interface included in its composition) (hereinafter — data and commands) intended for the operation of computers and mobile devices in order to obtain a specific result in the form of organizing the functionality of a social network. The set of data and commands consists of activated and non-activated data and commands.

**VK Mini Apps Platform** — an open platform of embeddable cross-platform applications that extend the capabilities of the VKontakte website and application.

**Customer** or **User** — a person possessing the necessary legal capacity to conclude the Agreement, who has created an Account on the VK Cloud Platform in accordance with clause 4 of these Rules. A Customer to whom the Company has granted the status of a Program Participant in accordance with the conditions listed in clause 4 of the Rules is also referred to as a "Program Participant".

**Customer Account** — information provided by the Customer (the availability of which is a necessary condition) for the registration and subsequent authorization of the Customer on the VK Cloud Platform in the process of the Company providing services to him, which includes a name (login), a password for accessing the Personal Account (password), as well as other information. Access to the Account is carried out through the Personal Account.

**Customer Personal Account** — a closed area of the VK Cloud Platform that allows the Customer to manage settings, edit the Customer Account, gain access to statistical data, as well as perform other actions necessary for the implementation of the functional capabilities of the VK Cloud Platform and individual Services.

**Customer Bonus Balance (account)** — a set of records reflecting the accrual/write-off by the Company of points to the Customer during advertising campaigns or when paying the Customer compensation in connection with a violation by the Company of the agreed service level. The Bonus Balance is reduced by the amount of services connected by the Customer on the VK Cloud Platform. If there are funds in both the Bonus and Personal Balance, write-offs are made first from the Bonus Balance, then from the Personal Balance.

**Customer Personal Balance (account)** — a set of records reflecting the financial relationship between the Customer and the Company regarding the services provided. The Customer's Balance increases by the amount of funds transferred by the Customer to the Company and credited by the Company to the Customer's Balance as prepayment for services, and decreases by the cost of services connected by the Customer.

**Zero Balance** — the absence of bonuses/funds in the Customer's Bonus and Personal Balance (account) for the Company to write off the payment for the services of providing access to the Services.

**Service** — a software module of the VK Cloud Platform that allows the implementation of individual functional capabilities within the VK Cloud Platform, which are provided by the Company or the Company's partners. Granting the Customer the right to use any Service does not depend on the provision of access to other Services.

**Project** — a set of logically interconnected web pages that are linked to a specific domain name or other identifier and operate according to the algorithms of a computer program. Within one Project, using the web interface of the VK Cloud Platform, Accounts for Users of the Project operating within such Project are created, who may be granted access to one or more Services of the VK Cloud Platform.

If the Rules use terms for which definitions are not given in this section of the Rules, the definitions of these terms from the Agreement are used.

## 2. Program Term

2.1. The VK Cloud program for supporting developers of VK Mini Apps services is valid from 27.03.2023 to 31.12.2023 inclusive.

## 3. Conditions of Participation in the Program

3.1. You can participate in the Program if you simultaneously:

- Are a developer of mini-applications on the VK Mini Apps Platform;
- Have created an Account on the VK Cloud Platform after the start of the Program in order to participate in it.

## 4. Application for Participation in the Program

4.1. To participate in the Program, you must:

- Create a new account on the VK Cloud Platform via the [link](https://cloud.vk.ru/?utm_source=partner&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=partner_mini-apps_2023-program) and receive a Project ID (created automatically and displayed in the upper left corner of the Personal Account). If you have any questions, contact the [support service](/en/contacts), where VK Cloud representatives will help you;
- Submit an application for participation in the Program (hereinafter — the "Application") by filling out a special form in the control panel of your mini-application on the moderation tab on the VK Mini Apps Platform, indicating the Project ID on the VK Cloud Platform;
- Wait for the Application to be approved to start working.

4.2. By submitting an Application for participation in the Program and pressing the "Submit application" button, the Participant, acting of their own will and in their own interest, gives their unconditional consent to the Company and the Partner to the Program Rules.

4.3. The Application is reviewed by the Company within 3 (three) business days. Based on the results of reviewing the Application and assessing the Customer's compliance with the Program criteria specified in section 3 of these Rules, the Company makes a decision to grant the Customer the status of a Program Participant or to refuse to grant such status. Information about the decision made is sent to the Customer's email address specified when submitting the Application.

## 5. Processing of Personal Data

5.1. By submitting an Application for participation in the Program, the User independently transfers to the Partner the Project ID on the VK Cloud Platform by entering the specified information into the Application form on the VK Mini Apps Platform. In turn, the Partner informs the Company whether the User is an active developer of services on the VK Mini Apps Platform (hereinafter — the "Indicator") by sending to the Company the Project ID received from the User and the Indicator.

5.2. For the purposes of fulfilling the Rules, in particular: providing the User with bonus points, the Company has the right to receive from the Partner, and the Partner — to transfer to the Company, and to process the personal data specified in clause 5.1, using and without using automation tools, in the following ways: recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, use, blocking, deletion, destruction of data.

5.3. The personal data specified in clause 5.1 of the Rules is stored by the Company for the duration of the Program Term, and after its expiration — for the period necessary and established by the current legislation of the Russian Federation.

## 6. Special Conditions for Program Participants

6.1. After completing registration on the VK Cloud Platform, 3000 ₽ (VAT inclusive) are credited to the Bonus Account, which can be spent until the end of the calendar month (until the expiration of the last day of the current calendar month) in which the Account was created. At the end of each calendar month, unused bonus points "burn out", and in subsequent calendar months during the Program Term, 2500 ₽ (VAT inclusive) are credited to the Bonus Account. Unused bonus points are not carried over to the next calendar month.

Upon expiration of the Program Term, unused bonus points accrued in the previous calendar month burn out.

## 7. What Bonus Points Can Be Spent On

7.1. Bonus points can be used for all Services available from the VK Cloud Personal Account within the basic quotas established by default for all new Projects. If necessary, quotas can be increased by contacting the support service from the Personal Account. About 30 infrastructure and platform services, modern tools for developing Cloud Native applications, as well as consultations with experts will be available.

## 8. Changes to the Rules. Termination of the Program

8.1. The norms of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation ("Civil Code of the RF") regulating the procedure and conditions for concluding an agreement by accepting a public offer apply to the form and method of concluding this agreement.

8.2. For the purposes of the Company fulfilling these Rules, the Program Participant agrees to receive informational messages from the Company.

8.3. The Company reserves the right to unilaterally make changes to the Rules with prior notification of the Program Participant at least 14 (fourteen) calendar days before the date of entry into force of the corresponding change by sending the relevant notification to the email address specified during registration of the Account on the VK Cloud Platform.

8.4. The new version of the Rules comes into force from the moment of its publication on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-vkma-program](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-vkma-program).

8.5. The Company has the right to unilaterally, with prior notification of the Customer, suspend or terminate the Rules and the Program at least 14 (fourteen) calendar days before the date of such termination by sending the relevant notification to the email address specified during registration of the Account on the VK Cloud Platform.

8.6. These Rules and the legal relations of the Parties in connection with the Program are governed by the legislation of the Russian Federation. All disputes in connection with these Rules shall be resolved in the manner provided for by the Agreement.
