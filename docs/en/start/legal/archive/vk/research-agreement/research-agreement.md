This Research (Testing) Participation Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the Agreement) is posted on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/research-agreement](/ru/start/legal/archive/vk/research-agreement) (hereinafter referred to as the link) and is available on the form page at [https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/](https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/) and is concluded only with those persons who have been previously provided with the said link on behalf of LLC "VK".

Taking into account that:

- LLC "VK" (hereinafter referred to as the Organizer) is a legal entity whose main activity is aimed at developing, implementing, and promoting services and products (including computer programs and mobile applications) in the field of the Internet.
- The Organizer is interested in conducting objective research, testing products and services to assess their attractiveness and ergonomics for Internet users, analyzing consumer properties, using the results of the research (testing) for the purpose of improving products and services, determining the Organizer's marketing strategy, conducting advertising campaigns, for scientific, informational, educational, and other similar purposes for which the research (testing) is conducted.

The Organizer invites individuals (hereinafter referred to as the Respondent) to voluntarily participate in the research (testing) of the Organizer's products and services.

## 1. Subject Matter and Other Terms

1.1. The Respondent undertakes to participate in the Organizer's research (testing) under the terms stipulated by this Agreement, and the Organizer undertakes to accept the results of the research (testing) from the Respondent.

1.2. The Respondent undertakes:

- to provide accurate and necessary information for the purpose of conducting a specific research (testing);
- to answer the Organizer's questions provided as part of the research (testing);
- to perform actions necessary for the purposes of conducting the research (testing);
- to complete tasks constituting the research (testing).

1.3. The Respondent's research (testing) may be conducted either online or with the Respondent's direct participation in the research (testing) at the Organizer's office.

1.4. The Organizer has the right to use the information received from the Respondent in its activities for the analysis and development of products and services, for conducting advertising/marketing campaigns, as well as for publication on the Internet and use in any public statements of the Organizer for informational, scientific, educational, and other similar purposes during the term of this Agreement, to disclose and use the results of the analysis of the information received from the Respondent in research results, analytical materials and reports, marketing research, advertising materials, and other similar materials in any form. At the same time, the Organizer undertakes not to use the information received from the Respondent in ways that damage his/her honor, dignity, or business reputation.

1.5. The Organizer undertakes to process the data received from the Respondent in accordance with the procedure defined by this Agreement and the legislation of the Russian Federation.

1.6. When undergoing the research (testing), the Respondent undertakes to take measures to ensure the security of his/her personal information or information of third parties that has become known to the Respondent, and to prevent third parties from accessing it. The Respondent bears independent responsibility for the disclosure of such information during the research (testing). At the same time, if during the research the Organizer accidentally becomes aware of the Respondent's personal information or the personal information of third parties for whom the Respondent has not taken security measures, the Organizer has the right to warn the Respondent of the need to comply with security measures. At the same time, the Organizer has no intention of using the said information.

1.7. The Respondent gives the Organizer consent to the processing of his/her personal data, namely: full name, contact information (phone number, email), and other information necessary for concluding and executing this Agreement with the Respondent, both using automation tools and without using automation tools, by the following methods: collection, systematization, accumulation, storage, refinement, use, transfer (dissemination, provision, access), blocking, depersonalization, destruction. The purpose of processing the Respondent's personal data is to conclude and fulfill obligations under this Agreement.

1.8. The Respondent understands and agrees that during the research (testing), the Organizer may record the Respondent's behavioral activity, which is due to the need to conduct the research (testing) and obtain objective results of the research (testing).

1.9. If the Respondent is a minor, this Agreement is concluded with his/her legal representative, and the legal representative must fill out the form at [https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/](https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/) with their own data and the Respondent's data.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1. The Parties have specifically stipulated that any information belonging to the Organizer and having actual or potential value for him/her due to its being unknown to third parties and the absence of free access to it on legal grounds, to which the Respondent may gain access in the process of participating in the research (testing), is confidential. Confidential information includes, but is not limited to:

a. any information received as part of participation in the research (testing) of the Organizer's products and services, including new ones;

b. technical information, including information about the Organizer's information systems, methodological and regulatory materials on the use and operation of information systems, research and testing reports, projects, drawings, information about materials used, production methods, object and source code of computer programs, as well as visualized images of the above information;

c. business information, including the Organizer's archival data, pricing procedures, sales strategy, marketing plans, research data;

d. strategic information, including proposals for new products, services, or markets; plans or negotiations on the acquisition and alienation of property;

e. information of third parties provided to the Organizer under confidentiality conditions: information about business activities, technologies, methods, software, as well as other data of the Organizer's counterparties, as well as affiliated persons and business partners of the Organizer, not publicly available;

f. other information and data that may have actual or potential value for the Organizer; information i) that has been designated by the Organizer as confidential, or ii) the circumstances of whose provision allow one to conclude that it is confidential, or iii) whose nature allows one to conclude that it is confidential.

2.2. The Respondent undertakes to comply with and take all measures within his/her power to protect the Organizer's confidential information and not to disclose it to third parties without the Organizer's consent.

2.3. Confidential Information always remains the property of the Organizer and must not be transferred to any third parties, copied, or otherwise reproduced or used in any way without the prior written consent of the transferring Party.

2.4. The obligation to keep Confidential Information secret in accordance with the terms of this Agreement comes into force from the moment the Agreement is signed by both Parties and remains in force for 10 (ten) years after the expiration of this Agreement or its early termination.

2.5. In case of disclosure of confidential information, the Respondent undertakes to compensate the Organizer for all losses caused as a result of such disclosure.

## 3. Other Terms

3.1. This Agreement is concluded by filling out the form posted at the link [https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/](https://cloud.vk.ru/uxlab/research_form/) and clicking the "Send" button located below the form. After performing these actions, the completed form will be automatically sent to the email address of LLC "VK" uxlab@mcs.mail.ru.

3.2. This Agreement is valid for 10 (ten) years from the moment the Respondent concludes the Agreement.

3.3. In cases of non-performance and/or improper performance of obligations under the Agreement, the Parties bear responsibility in accordance with the current legislation of the Russian Federation.

3.4. The Agreement may be terminated by agreement of the Parties or at the request of the Respondent by sending an appropriate notification to the address of the Organizer's location.
