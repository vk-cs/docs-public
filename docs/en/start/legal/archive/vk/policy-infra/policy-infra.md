This document is the official offer of LLC "VK" (hereinafter — the "Company") to conclude a user agreement (hereinafter — the "Agreement") with you (hereinafter — the "Customer") on the provision of services granting access to the Infra virtual infrastructure Service within the "VK Cloud" Platform (hereinafter — the "Platform"), on the terms set out in this Agreement, which is available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/en/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-infra](/en/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-infra).

## 1\. Terms and Definitions

**Website** — the internet website located at [https://cloud.vk.ru/](https://cloud.vk.ru/), which provides the Customer with access to the Service. The Company posts on the Website information that is mandatory for the Customer.

**"VK Cloud" Platform** (**Platform**) — a computer program operated by the Company and available to the Customer via the Website, which provides the Customer with access to the selected Services.

**Virtual Infrastructure Service (Service)** — a service of the Company known as the "Virtual Infrastructure Service" (Infra), consisting in providing the Customer with the ability to create virtual servers and virtual disks on the Company's servers.

**Content** — electronic documents, graphic images, photographs, scripts, texts, videos, music, sounds, computer programs, and other objects (files) uploaded by the Customer and hosted on the Service.

**Prohibited Content** — any Content described in clause 2.6. of this Agreement, as well as other materials whose use is prohibited by the Applicable Rules.

**Service Unavailability (Downtime)** — the time interval between the moment the Customer sends a message to the Company's support service by email to [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru) or via the feedback form in the management console, the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, and the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes restoration work.

**Compensation** — an amount of bonus points to be credited to the Customer's Bonus balance in the event of Downtime in the manner prescribed by this Agreement. Compensation does not involve the payment of monetary funds to the Customer.

**Reporting Period** — a calendar month.

**Applicable Rules** — the rules for using the Company's Service, available on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/en/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-infra](/en/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-infra), containing the terms of access to and use of the Service, set out in the following documents:

- Terms of Use of the "VK Cloud" Platform ([https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/vk/legal-terms](/en/start/legal/archive/vk/legal-terms));
- Company's Privacy Policy ([https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-privacy](/en/start/legal/archive/vk/policy-privacy)).

If this Agreement uses terms for which definitions are not provided in Section 1 of this Agreement, the definitions given to such terms in the Applicable Rules shall apply.

4.1. Use of the Services is paid. In order to determine the cost of using the Service, the Company establishes the price and the corresponding list of services in accordance with the Tariffs.

4.2. 6.4. The Company guarantees that the services of providing access to the Service are provided 24 (twenty-four) hours a day, 7 (seven) days a week.

6.5. The Company reserves the right to interrupt the operation of the Service to carry out necessary maintenance work, including on business days. Such cases will not be considered interruptions in the provision of services if the Company notifies the Customer in advance 24 (twenty-four) hours before the start of maintenance work causing interruptions in the operation of the Service, by publishing the relevant information on the Website (including the duration of the maintenance work). The time of Service unavailability related to the performance of maintenance work is not subject to compensation under the rules of clause 6.6. of the Agreement.

6.6. Service Unavailability ("Downtime") is defined as the time interval between the moment the Customer sends to the Company's support service a message via the feedback form available in the Personal Account, containing a notification of Service unavailability, or an email message to the address: [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru), the fact of which was subsequently confirmed by the Company, and the moment the Service resumes operation after the Company completes restoration work. If the Company has its own data on the start of the Downtime indicating an earlier start time of the Service unavailability than the time of sending the message by email to [support@mcs.mail.ru](mailto:support@mcs.mail.ru) or the feedback form in the management console, the Company may use such data. Disputes regarding the start and end time of the Downtime are resolved through negotiations between the Parties. If the Downtime began in one Reporting Period and ended in the next Reporting Period, the Service unavailability is entirely attributed to the Reporting Period in which the greater part of the Downtime occurred.

6.7. The amount of Compensation depends on the actual availability time of the Service in question and its cost debited from the Personal balance in the Reporting Period in which the Downtime was recorded.

For the purposes of calculating the Compensation, the cost of other services available on the Platform for which no Downtime was recorded is not taken into account.

In the event of Service unavailability, the Company undertakes, at the Customer's request, to transfer the Compensation to the Customer (credit it to the Bonus balance (account)), the amount of which is determined on the basis of the table below (hereinafter — Table No. 1):

| Availability time (in %)\* | Unavailability time (min or h) in one Reporting Period in total | Compensation amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 99.95% and above | less than 22 minutes | not paid |
| 99.9%–99.95% | from 22 to 45 minutes | 3% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 99.0%–99.9% | from 45 minutes to 7.5 hours | 10% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 95.0%–99.0% | from 7.5 hours to 36 hours | 20% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| 90.0%–95.0% | from 36 hours to 73 hours | 25% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |
| less than 90% | more than 73 hours | 50% of the amount debited from the Personal balance for the Service for the Reporting Period in which the Downtime occurred |

_\*The calculation is based on 43,800 minutes in a Reporting Period_

Within 10 (ten) business days from the date of receipt of the compensation request related to the Service unavailability, the Contractor undertakes to provide a response to the compensation request, which will specify the terms of providing the Customer with compensation for the Service unavailability or a reasoned refusal to provide it.

6.10. The amount of Compensation during a Reporting Period on both grounds specified in clauses 6.6. and 6.7. of the Agreement cannot exceed the amount of funds paid by the Customer for the Reporting Period in which the events serving as the basis for the Compensation occurred.

6.11. The following are not subject to Compensation: Service unavailability caused by the actions of the Customer or third parties, or by force majeure circumstances that resulted in the unavailability of services; suspension of the provision of services related to scheduled maintenance work, as defined in clause 6.5. of this Agreement; suspension of the provision of services at the request of state or municipal bodies in the cases and manner determined by applicable legislation. Under no circumstances does the Company compensate the Customer of the Service for indirect losses, including lost profit and damage to business reputation.
