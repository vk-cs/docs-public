- Crediting a welcome bonus to pay for the Platform services listed in clause 4.1 of these Rules, in an amount equivalent to up to 500,000 (five hundred thousand) rubles. The welcome bonus is valid for 2 months from the date the Customer is granted the status of Promotion Participant.

3.3.2. In case of choosing the "Multicloud" Program:

- Crediting a welcome bonus to pay for the Platform services listed in clause 4.1 of these Rules, in an amount equivalent to up to 500,000 (five hundred thousand) rubles. The welcome bonus is valid for 2 months from the date the Customer is granted the status of Promotion Participant.

- A discount on the payment for the Professional Services team's consulting services for improving the fault tolerance of solutions in the cloud or building a "second shoulder" on the VK Cloud platform.

3.3.3. In case of choosing the "Cloud-Native Heroes" Program:

- Crediting a welcome bonus to pay for the Platform services listed in clause 4.1 of these Rules, in an amount equivalent to up to 800,000 (eight hundred thousand) rubles. The specified amount may be used within 3 (three) months exclusively in the following order: during the first month, 50% of the standard cost of the Platform services may be paid with bonus rubles; during the second month — up to 100% of the standard cost of the Platform services; during the third month — 50% of the standard cost of the Platform services.
