## 3. Terms of Participation in the Promotion

3.1. To participate in the Promotion, the Participant must perform the following actions/meet the following criteria:

3.1.1. have a management console (be registered) on the VK Cloud platform ([https://cloud.vk.ru/](https://cloud.vk.ru/));

3.1.2. join the Promotion through active actions (by contacting the support service, or via the feedback form on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/form/](https://cloud.vk.ru/form/));

3.1.3. purchase and launch the Cloud Desktop service ([https://cloud.vk.ru/cloud-desktop/](https://cloud.vk.ru/cloud-desktop/)) for the first time, and create at least one remote desktop;

3.1.4. have not previously used the Cloud Desktop service ([https://cloud.vk.ru/cloud-desktop/](https://cloud.vk.ru/cloud-desktop/)).

3.2. Only Participants who have performed the actions specified in clause 3.1 of the Promotion Rules are eligible to receive the Prize. The Organizer may refuse participation in the Promotion if a violation of the terms of the Promotion Rules by the Participant is established.

3.3. Repeated participation in the Promotion is not allowed.

5.1. The number of Winners is unlimited.
