## 3. Conditions of Participation in the Promotion and the Procedure for Conducting the Promotion

3.1. To take part in the Promotion, the Participant must perform the following actions / meet the following criteria:

3.1.1. The Promotion Participant must be a legal entity or individual entrepreneur who has confirmed their status in the manner provided on the page [https://cloud.vk.ru/bonus/](https://cloud.vk.ru/bonus/).

3.1.2. The Promotion may be joined by Participants who have not used the Services on a paid basis (including, have not paid for the Services with bonuses) before submitting an application to participate in the Promotion.

3.1.3. The Promotion Participant must submit an application to participate in the Promotion within the Promotion Period.

3.1.4. The Promotion Participant intends to use VK Cloud services to solve their IT tasks.

By submitting an application, the Participant unconditionally and fully agrees to these Rules.

3.2. Only Participants who have performed the actions / meet the criteria specified in clause 3.1 of the Promotion Rules may obtain the right to participate in the Promotion. The Organizer has the right, at its own discretion and without explanation, to refuse participation in the Promotion, including but not limited to cases where a violation of the Promotion Rules by the Participant is established.

The Organizer has the right, solely at its own discretion, to exclude the Participant from participation in the Promotion and to refuse to grant the special terms of use of the Platform, including refusing to extend support to the subsequent stages.

**First stage (within 30 calendar days from the date of approval of the Participant's application)**:

- Crediting of Bonus points to pay for the Platform Services (except for those listed in clause 4.1 of these Rules) in an amount equivalent to up to 150,000 (one hundred fifty thousand) rubles. The specific amount of bonus points is determined by the Organizer before the Participant starts using the Services and is credited to the Participant individually based on an assessment of the Participant's needs and the project being implemented, but in any case cannot exceed 150,000 (one hundred fifty thousand) rubles. The Bonus is valid for 30 calendar days from the date of approval of the application to participate in the Promotion. After the specified period, unused Bonus points expire.

- Migration using the "Hosted Solution Acure – migration" tools in lift-and-shift mode (transfer without changing the architecture) under the terms specified on the page: [https://cloud.vk.ru/migration/](https://cloud.vk.ru/migration/).

**Second stage (within 60 calendar days from the date of completion of the First stage)**:

- Granting the Participant special price terms for the use of the Services in the form of a 50% reduction of the standard rates for the Services specified in the Price List ([https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist)). These special price terms do not apply to the Services specified in clause 4.1 of these Rules.

- Migration using the "Hosted Solution Acure – migration" tools in lift-and-shift mode (transfer without changing the architecture) under the terms specified on the page: [https://cloud.vk.ru/migration/](https://cloud.vk.ru/migration/).

- From the date of completion of the Second stage, payment for the Services is made by the Participant at the standard rates specified in the Price List (if, at the end of the Second stage, the Organizer does not decide to extend support to the Participant).  

**Third stage (within 30 calendar days from the date of completion of the Second stage, applicable if the Organizer decides to extend support):**

- Granting the Participant special price terms for the use of the Services in the form of a 30% reduction of the standard rates for the Services specified in the Price List ([https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist)). These special price terms do not apply to the Services specified in clause 4.1 of these Rules.

- Migration using the "Hosted Solution Acure – migration" tools in lift-and-shift mode (transfer without changing the architecture) under the terms specified on the page: [https://cloud.vk.ru/migration/](https://cloud.vk.ru/migration/).

- The Organizer reserves the right, at its own discretion, not to extend support to the Participant for the Third stage.

- From the date of completion of the Third stage, payment for the Services is made by the Participant at the standard rates specified in the Price List.
