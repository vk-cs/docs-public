The Startup Support Program (hereinafter — the **"Program"**) has been developed by Digital Cloud Limited Liability Company (OGRN: 1197746546568) and VK Digital Technologies Limited Liability Company (OGRN: 5177746017158) and is intended to attract and retain on the "VK Cloud" and "VK WorkSpace" Platforms companies (legal entities or individual entrepreneurs) that develop digital products and services.

## 1. Terms and definitions

**Program** — a startup support program providing special conditions for using the VK Cloud, VK WorkSpace, and VK Ads platforms (hereinafter — collectively, the Platforms) for Program Participants.

**Rules** — this document, which is a public offer, posted on the Internet at: [https://cloud.vk.ru/docs/ru/start/legal/loyalty-programs/policy-startup-program](/en/start/legal/loyalty-programs/policy-startup-program).

**Program Term** — the period during which the Program is valid and applications for participation in the Program may be submitted.

**Organizer-1** — Digital Cloud LLC.

**Organizer-2** — VK Digital Technologies LLC.

When mentioned jointly, Organizer-1 and Organizer-2 may be referred to as the "Organizers", and individually as the "Organizer".

**VK Cloud Platform / VK Cloud** — a computer program available to the User via the website [https://cloud.vk.ru/](https://cloud.vk.ru/) that allows the User, in accordance with the terms of the Agreement, to create and delete Projects, create and delete within such Projects Accounts for the Users of the Projects, as well as grant access to and restrict such Users' access to selected Services.

**VK WorkSpace Platform / VK WorkSpace** — a computer program available to the user via the website https://workspace.vk.ru that allows the user, in accordance with the terms of the agreement on the VK WorkSpace Platform, to create and delete projects, create and delete within such projects accounts for end users of the projects, as well as grant access to and restrict such end users' access to selected VK WorkSpace services.

**VK Ads Platform / VK Ads** — a software and hardware complex created for the purpose of providing advertising placement services, located at [https://ads.vk.ru/](https://ads.vk.ru/), including one that provides the advertiser with the ability to independently place advertising materials, exercise control, and maintain statistical records of the placed advertising materials. The rights holder of the VK Ads Platform and a partner within the framework of this Program is VK LLC.

**User** — a person with the necessary legal capacity who has created an Account on the VK Cloud Platform or on the VK WorkSpace Platform and has concluded an agreement with the Organizers for the use of the services of the respective Platforms.

A User who has been granted the status of a Program Participant by one of the Organizers in accordance with the conditions listed in Section 3 of the Rules is also referred to as a "Program Participant".

**Price List** — the web page [https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist) indicating the cost of the VK Cloud Platform Services.

**VK Cloud Service** — a software module of the VK Cloud Platform that enables the implementation, within the VK Cloud Platform, of individual functional capabilities provided by Organizer-1 or Organizer-1's partners. Granting the User the right to use any Service does not depend on the provision of access to other VK Cloud Services.

**VK WorkSpace Service** — computer programs whose rights holder is Organizer-2, the right to use of which is granted to the User under the terms of the relevant agreement concluded with Organizer-2.

If the Rules use terms for which definitions are not given in this Section of the Rules, the definitions of such terms from the agreements on the use of the services of the VK WorkSpace, VK Cloud, and VK Ads Platforms, accepted by the User on the websites of the respective Platforms, shall apply.

## 2. Program Term

2.1. The Program is valid from August 7, 2023 to December 31, 2026 inclusive. During the specified period, the User has the right to submit an application to participate in the Program. The special conditions for using the Platforms provided for in Section 5 of the Rules are valid for 6 (six) months from the date the User is granted the status of Program Participant.

## 3. Conditions of Participation in the Program

3.1. You can participate in the Program if you have performed the actions listed below / meet the following criteria:

- You are developing a digital product or service (a product/service at the MVP stage or above) and are its rights holder (the exclusive right to the product belongs to you).
- You have submitted an application to participate in the Program in the manner provided for in Section 4 of these Rules and have received confirmation of participation from the Program Organizer.

3.2. To apply the special conditions for using VK Cloud services listed in clause 5.1 of the Rules, the Program Participant must be a new User of VK Cloud Services, which means that the User has not previously had an Account on VK Cloud, has registered an Account on the VK Cloud Platform no earlier than "01" January 2026, and has not paid for Services of access to the Services at the time of submitting the application to participate in the Program. At the same time, the Program Participant's account on the VK Cloud Platform must be registered as a legal entity or individual entrepreneur.

3.3. To apply the special conditions for using VK Ads and VK WorkSpace services listed in clauses 5.2 and 5.3 of the Rules, the Program Participant must be a new user of VK Ads and VK WorkSpace services (you are a new user if, at the time of being granted participant status in the Program, you have never paid for the services of the specified services). The Program Participant on the VK Ads Platform must be registered as a direct advertiser, and on the VK WorkSpace Platform, the Program Participant's account must be registered as a legal entity or individual entrepreneur.

3.4. **Restrictions**. Integrators, digital agencies implementing projects for third-party client companies, and developers of products and services commissioned by companies that are their rights holders cannot join the Program.

## 4. Application for Participation in the Program

4.1. To participate in the Program, you must submit an application on the Website [https://cloud.vk.ru/startup](https://cloud.vk.ru/startup) or [https://workspace.vk.ru/for-startups/?erid=2SDnjeJvme2](https://workspace.vk.ru/for-startups/?erid=2SDnjeJvme2). Applications can be submitted until December 31, 2026 inclusive.

4.2. **By submitting an application to participate in the Program and clicking the "Submit application" button, the User, acting of their own free will and in their own interest, expresses their full and unconditional agreement with the Program Rules.**

4.3. The application is reviewed by the Organizer within 5 (five) business days. Based on the results of reviewing the application and assessing the User's compliance with the Program criteria specified in Section 3 of these Rules, the Organizer makes a decision to grant the User the status of Program Participant or to refuse to grant such status. The User unconditionally agrees that the Organizer reserves the right to refuse to grant the User the status of Program Participant at its own discretion without giving reasons.

Information about the decision made is sent to the User's email address specified when submitting the application.

## 5. Special Conditions for Using the Platforms for Program Participants

### 5.1. Special conditions for using VK Cloud services

5.1.1. Within 6 (six) months from the date of approval of the Application for participation in the Program, the Program Participant is granted access to the services specified in clause 5.1.2 on the VK Cloud Platform under special conditions:

- **First month of participation in the Program:** accrual of bonus points for payment of VK Cloud services (listed in clause 5.1.2 of these Rules) in an amount equivalent to up to 250,000 (two hundred fifty thousand) rubles. The specific amount of bonus points is determined by Organizer-1 and accrued to the Participant individually based on an assessment of the Participant's needs and the project being implemented, but in any case cannot exceed 250,000 (two hundred fifty thousand) rubles. Bonus points are valid for 1 (one) month and are not carried over to the next period. After the specified period, unused bonus points expire.

- **Second month of participation in the Program:** accrual of bonus points for payment of VK Cloud services (listed in clause 5.1.2 of these Rules) in an amount equivalent to up to 250,000 (two hundred fifty thousand) rubles. The specific amount of bonus points is determined by Organizer-1 and accrued to the Participant individually based on an assessment of the Participant's needs and the project being implemented, but in any case cannot exceed 250,000 (two hundred fifty thousand) rubles. Bonus points are valid for 1 (one) month and are not carried over to the next period. After the specified period, unused Bonus points expire.

- **Third through sixth month of participation in the Program:** starting from the third month of participation in the Program, the Participant is granted special price conditions for using VK Cloud Services in the form of a 50% reduction of the standard rates for the VK Cloud services specified in clause 5.1.2 of these Rules.

For the purposes of these Rules, a month of participation in the Program means a period equal to 30 (thirty) calendar days.

5.1.2. Restrictions on the application of special conditions within VK Cloud

- The special conditions apply to the following VK Cloud Services: Cloud Servers, Cloud Storage, Managed Kubernetes, Cloud Databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL, ClickHouse), Cloud Spark, Cloud Trino, Cloud ML Platform, Cloud Backup.

  Payment for the use of other Services is made by the User on general terms based on the prices specified in the Price List.

- Bonus points not used by the Participant during the month in which they were accrued expire.
- Bonus points can be used exclusively to pay for the above VK Cloud Services and cannot be withdrawn by the Participant in cash equivalent and/or transferred to a third party.
- The maximum amount of benefits used by the Participant, including the amount of the 50% discount and the amounts of bonus points accrued to the Participant, for the entire period of the Participant's participation in the Program is **no more than 2,000,000 (two million rubles), including VAT**. If this amount is exceeded, payment for the use of the Services is made by the User on general terms based on the prices specified in the Price List.
- After the expiration of the support periods specified in clause 5.1.1 of these Rules, payment for VK Cloud Services is made by the Participant on general terms at the standard rates specified in the Price List.

5.1.2.
Consultation on the use of cloud services for hosting the User's product on the VK Cloud Platform (one consultation from a VK Cloud expert).

5.1.3. Free basic technical support in accordance with the terms posted on the website [https://cloud.vk.ru/](https://cloud.vk.ru).

5.2. Special conditions for using the services of the VK WorkSpace Platform

The Program Participant is granted the right to use VK WorkSpace services under the "Basic" plan with a 50% discount (description of the plan and other conditions are available at the link [https://workspace.vk.ru/tariffs/](https://workspace.vk.ru/tariffs)) for 6 months. The plan includes the following set of VK WorkSpace tools: business email, calendar, messenger with video calls, drive, and document editor.

The right to use the discount is provided by issuing a promo code to the Participant. The promo code is available for use if the Participant chooses monthly payment for services and is not applicable if the Participant chooses an annual service usage plan.

Activation of the promo code occurs in the Participant's management console by entering the promo code in a special field and subsequently topping up the balance of the Participant's management console with funds. To apply the discount, the Participant must enter the promo code in the management console on the VK WorkSpace Platform each time the license is renewed for the next reporting monthly period.

The Program Participant has the right to use the received promo code no more than 6 (six) times during the validity period of the promo code.

**The promo code is valid from the date of its activation until December 31, 2026.** The promo code is annulled if it is not activated by the Program Participant within the specified period. After the expiration of the specified period, the use of the promo code (including repeated use) becomes unavailable.

The Participant is not entitled to:

- withdraw the cash equivalent of the discount amount (promo code) from the management console balance;
- transfer the right to receive the discount (promo code) to another Participant/third party, or transfer the discount amount (promo code) to the balance of another management console.

After the promo code is exhausted or expires, the Participant chooses a VK WorkSpace service usage plan available on the website [https://workspace.vk.ru/tariffs/](https://workspace.vk.ru/tariffs/) and pays for it in full under standard conditions.

### 5.3. Special conditions for using the services of the VK Ads Platform

5.3.1. A VK Ads promo code is a special set of characters (letters and/or digits and/or other signs), the activation of which provides for receiving (accruing) a discount in the form of bonuses, provided that the balance of the Program Participant's management console on the VK Ads service [https://ads.vk.ru/](https://ads.vk.ru/) (hereinafter — the **Promo Code**) is topped up with funds in the amount specified in these Rules.

The Program Participant is granted a Promo Code with a nominal value of 25,000 (twenty-five thousand) bonus units (1 bonus unit = 1 ruble) when topping up the balance of the Program Participant's management console with funds in a single payment in the amount of at least 25,000 (twenty-five thousand) rubles (excluding VAT) after activation of the Promo Code.

Bonuses are valid for 60 (sixty) calendar days from the date they are provided. After the expiration of the specified period, unused bonuses expire.

Activation of the Promo Code occurs in the Participant's management console by entering the Promo Code in a special field.

The Program Participant has the right to top up the management console balance with funds after activation of the Promo Code 1 (one time) within the period specified when receiving the Promo Code, but in any case no later than 3 (three) months from the moment of its receipt. The Promo Code is annulled if it is not used by the Program Participant within the specified period.

Bonus funds provided under the Promo Code can be spent by the Participant only on payment for placement services provided using VK Ads. The Participant is not entitled to:

- withdraw the cash equivalent of the discount amount (Promo Code) from the management console balance;
- transfer the right to receive the discount (Promo Code) to another Participant/third party, or transfer the discount amount (Promo Code) to the balance of another advertising account.

The Participant can receive the Promo Code only after 1 (one) calendar month of using VK Cloud services, that is, from the second month of participation in the Program. Without using VK Cloud services for at least 1 month — the Promo Code will be unavailable to the Program Participant.

5.3.2. Upon request, the Program Participant is provided with targeting specialist support for 3 (three) months from the date of being granted Participant status: recommendations on setting up the parameters of one VK Ads advertising campaign, audit and adjustment of such advertising campaign.

Upon expiration of 6 (six) months from the date the Participant joined the Program, the User loses the right to apply all special conditions for using the Platforms listed in clauses 5.1–5.3 of these Rules, regardless of their actual use.

## 7. Exclusion from the Program

A User may be deprived of the status of Program Participant in the event of any of the following circumstances:

- The User did not start using the special conditions on VK Cloud or VK WorkSpace within the first 2 (two) months from the moment of joining the Program. In the specified case, the Organizer notifies the User of the termination of Participant status by sending a notification to the Participant's email address specified when submitting the application, 14 (fourteen) calendar days before the date of exclusion from the Program.
- Suspicious activity has been detected in the User's Project, or the Project does not comply with the Conditions of Participation in the Program. In the specified case, the Organizer notifies the User of the termination of Participant status by sending a notification to the Participant's email address specified when submitting the application, 7 (seven) calendar days before the date of exclusion from the Program.

From the moment the User is excluded from the Program, the special conditions for using VK Cloud, VK WorkSpace, and VK Ads provided for in Section 5 of these Rules terminate. Starting from the day following the day of the User's exclusion from the Program, payment for services on the VK Cloud and VK WorkSpace Platforms is made on the basis of the rates published on the websites of the Platforms. VK Ads promo codes, both activated and not activated at the time of the User's exclusion from the Program, are annulled.
