## 3. Conditions of Participation in the Program and Procedure for Its Conduct

3.1. To participate in the Program, the Participant must perform the following actions / meet the following criteria:

3.1.1. The Participant must be a legal entity or individual entrepreneur registered on the territory of the Russian Federation and have a valid status of winner of one or more competitions held by the Institute of Internet Development in the "Software Products" category.

3.1.2. The Participant must fill out the application form for participation in the Program on the one-page presentation, providing confirmation of winning at the Institute of Internet Development (a copy of the contract with the Institute of Internet Development and/or another official document confirming victory in the competition in the "Software Products" category).

3.1.3. By submitting an application, the Participant unconditionally and fully accepts these Rules, as well as the Terms of Use of the VK Cloud platform located at: [https://cloud.vk.com/docs/start/legal/offer/offer-agreement](/ru/start/legal/offer/offer-agreement). From the moment the Participant starts using VK Cloud services, the Participant is deemed to have concluded an agreement with the Organizer under the terms specified in the Terms of Use. No separate signing of the agreement is required.

3.1.4. Confirm that the Project for which participation in the Program is requested is in the active development stage. The Participant guarantees that the purposes of using the VK Cloud infrastructure are related to the development, testing, or launch of new features of the Project, and not solely to the technical support of an already completed and delivered product. The Program does not apply to projects for which obligations to the Institute of Internet Development have been fully fulfilled and the certificates of completed works have been signed, unless such a project involves further significant development (scaling, porting, etc.).

3.1.5. Participation in the Program is granted provided that the Participant has concluded a contract with the Institute of Internet Development as a result of the competition specified in clause 3.1.1. If the Participant refuses to conclude such a contract, Bonuses are not provided. If the concluded contract is terminated after the Bonuses have been activated, all unused Bonuses are canceled (written off) from the date of termination, and the granted Discount ceases to be effective on the same date.

3.2. The right to participate in the Program is available only to Participants who have performed the actions / meet the criteria specified in clause 3.1 of these Rules. The Organizer has the right, at its sole discretion and without explanation, to refuse participation in the Program, including, but not limited to, cases where a violation of these Rules or the Terms of Use by the Participant is established.

3.3. If the conditions specified in clause 3.1 of these Rules are met, the Participant is granted the following special terms of use of the VK Cloud Platform:

3.3.1. **Infrastructure grant**

- Grant limit: up to 1,000,000 (one million) bonus rubles (bonuses) to the VK Cloud balance.
- Grant usage mechanics: during the first 3 (three) months from the date of Program activation, bonuses cover up to 50% of the monthly consumption of basic infrastructure services within the total limit. The remaining portion is paid by the Participant at standard VK Cloud rates.
- Grant usage period: bonuses are credited after connection to the Program and can be used within 3 (three) months from the activation date. The unused remainder is canceled.

3.3.2. **Long-term discount**

- After the first 3 (three) months, the Participant is granted a 30% (thirty percent) discount on basic infrastructure services.
- Discount validity period: 24 (twenty-four) months from the end of the grant period.
- The discount is applied to actual consumption and is not combined with other special offers.

3.3.3. **Technological support**

- Recommendations on architecture and scaling of projects.
- For simple scenarios, free migration in lift-and-shift mode (transfer without architecture changes) is available using the "Hystax Acura — Migration" tool.
- Assistance in fulfilling the KPIs of grant projects of the Institute of Internet Development (preparation of transparent reporting, consultations).
- Priority consideration of applications for placement in RuStore (accelerated moderation) and additional support for projects on the showcase (at the discretion of the RuStore editorial team). The ability to publish on VK Play and use mini-apps in the MAX messenger.
- A dedicated VK Cloud manager to support the project.

## 4. Restrictions on the Application of Special Terms

4.1. Bonuses provided under the Program cannot be used to pay for:

- GPU resources (virtual machines with graphics accelerators);
- licensed software (including Marketplace commercial products);
- technical support services beyond the standard package;
- other services not included in the list of basic infrastructure services specified in clause 4.2.

4.2. Bonuses and the discount apply to the following basic infrastructure services of the VK Cloud Platform:

- Cloud Servers (virtual machines);
- VK Object Storage (object storage);
- Managed Kubernetes;
- Managed Databases (all types of DBMS);
- Cloud Load Balancers and network services;
- Dedicated IP addresses;
- Open-source solutions from the Marketplace that do not require license purchases.

4.3. The maximum amount of the grant (bonuses) available to one Participant under the Program is 1,000,000 (one million) rubles. The specific amount of credited bonuses is determined by the Organizer individually but cannot exceed the specified limit.

4.4. Bonuses credited under this Program are not combined with other special terms provided to the Participant. The Participant cannot simultaneously be a participant and use the benefits provided under other programs and promotions of the Organizer, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4.5. Upon expiration of the grant period (3 months), unused bonuses are canceled. Bonuses cannot be withdrawn by the Participant in monetary equivalent and/or transferred to a third party.

4.6. The 30% discount is granted only on the condition of active use of the services by the Participant during the grant period. The Organizer has the right to refuse to grant the discount if the Participant's consumption during the grant period was zero or insignificant (less than 10,000 rubles per month).

4.7. Services for placement in a data center on the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan are provided subject to technical feasibility and may be limited by the Organizer.
