The response and resolution time of a request depends on the urgency and impact of the request on clients' information resources. Below are the tables of response and resolution times for all types of requests:

## {heading(Incident classification)[id=support-sla-classification]}

### {heading(Incident classification table)[id=support-sla-classification-table]}

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Incident severity level
| Problem description

| Critical
|* The Customer's business processes are blocked: services do not work / are not deployed / operate in a mode of significant degradation.
* The detected problem potentially threatens the confidentiality, availability, and integrity of the Customer's information.
* The detected problem leads to a violation of legislative requirements regarding the protection of confidential information.

| High
| The Customer's business processes operate with significant degradation: significant degradation / slowdown of components and/or Services.

| Medium
| The Customer's business processes operate normally: minor degradation / slowdown of components or Services.
Services continue to operate without restrictions.

| Low
| Detection of errors and problems that do not lead to a shutdown or degradation of components and Services.

|===

The request types **Service Request** and **Request for Information** are equated to low-severity incidents.

### {heading(Request description table)[id=support-sla-request]}

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Name
| Description 

| Service Request
| Requests for support of service operation: creation/modification/enabling/disabling of services, settings, entities.

| Request for Information
| Consultation on service settings and operation<br>within the scope of technical support.

|===

### {heading(Response time)[id=support-sla-reaction-time]}

The response time for a request is no more than 60 minutes.

{note:info}

The countdown of the response time for a request starts from the moment the Customer is notified of its registration and assignment of an identification number until the moment of the first response from the technical support service.

{/note}

### {heading(Resolution statistics table)[id=support-sla-stats]}

| Severity level | Resolution time|
|------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Critical      | 95% of requests with critical priority are resolved within 2 hours.|
| High          | 95% of requests with high priority are resolved within 12 hours.|
| Medium          | 95% of requests with medium priority are resolved within 24 hours.|
| Low           | 95% of requests with low priority are resolved within 48 hours.|
