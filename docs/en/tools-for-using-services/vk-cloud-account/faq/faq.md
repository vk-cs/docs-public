# {heading(Frequently asked questions)[id=vk-cloud-account-faq]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

{cut(What should I do if the email confirmation request appears again?)}

Before the next email confirmation attempt, try:

- Clearing the cache and cookie files in your browser.
- Using incognito mode or a different browser.

If the problem persists, [contact technical support](https://cloud.vk.ru/contacts/).

{/cut}

{cut(What should I do if emails are not being received?)}

1. Check the Spam folder in your mailbox.
1. Make sure you are using the email address the account was registered with.

If the problem persists, [contact technical support](https://cloud.vk.ru/contacts/).

{/cut}

{cut(How do I change the account owner's email?)}

If you do not have access to the account owner's email address, [contact technical support](/en/contacts) for identification and email change.

{/cut}

{cut(How do I reset my account password?)}

{linkto(../instructions/account-manage/editinfo#account-manage-editinfo-password)[text=Change]} your account password.

If you do not remember your current password, [contact technical support](https://cloud.vk.ru/contacts/) for identification and access recovery.

{/cut}

{cut(What should I do if both the password and the phone are lost?)}

1. [Use](https://cloud.vk.ru/authapp/password/recovery/forward) the password recovery form on the login page.
1. If phone-based recovery is not possible, [contact technical support](/en/contacts), providing the data for identifying the account owner (passport details, contract details, information about recent payments).

{/cut}

{cut(What should I do if the 2FA code is invalid?)}

1. Make sure the exact time is set on your device.

1. Generate the code immediately before entering it. If the code has been refreshed, enter the new one.

1. Check that the entry in the app belongs specifically to {var(cloud)}, not to another service.

1. Use the backup codes saved during 2FA setup.

1. If the problem persists or the backup codes are missing or lost, [contact technical support](/en/contacts).

{/cut}

{cut(What should I do if access to the account is lost due to the absence of a 2FA device?)}

1. Use the backup codes saved during 2FA setup.
1. If the codes are lost, [contact technical support](/en/contacts), providing the data for identifying the account owner (passport details, contract details, information about recent payments).

{/cut}

{cut(How do I get an account with the cloud.vk.ru domain if I only have one with mcs.mail.ru?)}

The platforms with the `mcs.mail.ru` and `cloud.vk.ru` domains have been merged. To log in to your {var(cloud)} account, use your previous email and password.

If you have access issues, [contact technical support](https://cloud.vk.ru/contacts/).

{/cut}

{cut(How do I configure single sign-on to the technical support portal for federated users?)}

Contact [technical support](/en/contacts). In your request, specify:

- the ID of your identity federation;
- the list of trusted domains of your organization.

For more information on configuring single sign-on to the technical support portal, see the {linkto(../../../access/iam/instructions/support-fim#iam-support-fim)[text=instructions]}.

{/cut}
