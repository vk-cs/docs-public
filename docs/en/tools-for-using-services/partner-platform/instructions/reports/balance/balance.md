# {heading(Balance and resource consumption details)[id=partner-platform-balance]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

You can view resource consumption statistics and balance changes for each customer {linkto(../../../../account/concepts/projects#tools-account-concepts-projects)[text=project]}.
The details can be downloaded as a report.

## {heading(Viewing project balance)[id=pp-balance-view]}

1. [Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/partnerapp) the VK Tech partner dashboard.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Dashboard** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Customers** section.
1. Click the name of the desired customer in the list.
1. Go to the **Projects** tab.
1. Click the project name. The {linkto(../../../../../intro/billing/concepts/balance#billing-balance)[text=balance]} of the project's {linkto(../../../../account/concepts/projects#tools-account-concepts-projects)[text=project]} personal and bonus accounts will be displayed on the right side of the screen.
1. Click **Service bonuses** to view the remaining amount and expiration date of {linkto(../../../../../intro/billing/concepts/balance#billing-balance-bonuses)[text=bonuses]} for each group of the bonus account. The bonus breakdown is displayed if the customer has separate bonuses on the project's bonus account for virtual machines and for other service groups.

## {heading(Viewing project balance details)[id=pp-balance-view-detail]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner dashboard.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Dashboard** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Customers** section.
1. Click the name of the desired customer in the list.
1. Go to the **Projects** tab.
1. Click the project name. The **Details** tab will open.
1. (Optional) Set the filters:

   - The period for which the details are generated.
   - Level of detail: day, week, month, or year.
   - Type of funds movement: charges or credits.

1. (Optional) Click the row with the name of a specific service to view its details.

## {heading(Viewing resource consumption statistics)[id=pp-balance-view-stats]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner dashboard.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Dashboard** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Customers** section.
1. Click the name of the desired customer in the list.
1. Go to the **Projects** tab.
1. Click the project name.
1. Go to the **Service Expenses** tab.
1. (Optional) Set the filters:

   - The period for which the details are generated.
   - Level of detail: day, week, month, or year.

## {heading(Downloading a report)[id=pp-balance-download]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner dashboard.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Dashboard** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Customers** section.
1. Click the name of the desired customer in the list.
1. Go to the **Projects** tab.
1. Click the project name. The **Details** tab will open.
1. (Optional) Set the filters:

   - The period for which the details are generated.
   - Level of detail: day, week, month, or year.
   - Type of funds movement: charges or credits.

1. Click the icon ![Download](./assets/download_icon.svg "inline").
1. In the window that opens, select the type of report to download: **docx**, **xlsx**, **For accounting**.
1. Click the **Download report** button.
