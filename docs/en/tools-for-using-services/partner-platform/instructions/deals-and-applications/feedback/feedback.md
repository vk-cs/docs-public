# {heading(Working with feedback)[id=pp-feedback]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

Under the terms of the partner program, the partner sends feedback to {var(cloud)} on registered deals at least once a month.

## {heading(Sending feedback)[id=pp-feedback-send]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner management console.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Deal Registration** section, to the **Confirmed Registrations** tab.
1. Open the feedback form in one of the following ways:

   - Click ![ ](../../../../../assets/more-icon.svg "inline") for the desired deal and select **Leave feedback**.
   - Click on the desired deal and, in the panel that opens, click **Leave feedback**.

1. Fill in the feedback form:

   1. Select the feedback type:

      - `Monthly status`: a regular deal status without renewal or closure.
      - `Renewal`: feedback and a request to renew the registration according to the sales model (SaaS/OnPrem).
      - `Closure`: the deal registration is removed; the deal is closed by the seller.

   1. (Optional) Select the closure type. The list is displayed if you previously selected `Closure`.
   1. Select the feedback category that reflects the current deal status:

      - `Pilot / Testing`;
      - `In progress / Implementation / Integration`;
      - `Awaiting decision / Approval / Budget`;
      - `Sold`;
      - `Rejected / Not relevant / Closed (unsuccessfully)`.

   1. Leave a comment on the feedback in free form.
   1. Select the risks: you can select more than one risk.
   1. Leave a comment on the list of risks.
   1. Select the competitors: you can select more than one competitor.
   1. Leave a comment on the list of competitors.

1. Click the **Submit** button.

## {heading(Viewing feedback)[id=pp-feedback-view]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner management console.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Deal Registration** section, to the **Confirmed Registrations** tab.
1. Click on the desired deal.

   The data from the last feedback about the deal sent to {var(cloud)} will be displayed in the **Latest Information** block.
