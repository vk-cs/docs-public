# {heading(Deal registration)[id=pp-deal-registration]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner management console.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Open the application creation form in one of the following ways:

   - On the main page, click the **New application** button.
   - Go to the **Deal registration** section and click the **New application** button.

1. Expand the **Partner information** block and make sure that the **Partner name** and **INN** fields contain the correct data of your company.

   If there are errors in the data, contact [technical support](/en/contacts). You can register the deal right away without waiting for the inaccuracies to be corrected.

1. Provide the details of contact persons on your side:

   1. In the **Contact for receiving registration response** section, provide the details of your employee responsible for tracking deal registration applications.
   1. (Optional) To specify multiple phone numbers or email addresses of the responsible employee, click **One more phone** and **One more address**.
   1. In the **Account manager on the partner side** section, provide the details of the customer relationship manager on your side.

      If this manager is also responsible for tracking applications, enable the **Matches the registration confirmation contact** option. In this case, the form fields will be automatically filled in with the data from the previous section.

   1. (Optional) To specify multiple phone numbers or email addresses of the manager, click **One more phone** and **One more address**.

1. Expand the **Customer information** block and provide the details of the company whose deal you want to register:

   1. In the **Customer name** field, enter the name of the customer company.
   1. In the **INN** field, enter the INN of this company.
   1. In the **Contact of the decision-maker on the customer side** section, provide the details of the customer employee responsible for concluding deals.

1. Expand the **Deal information** block and provide the parameters of the deal you want to register:

   1. In the **MCS ID** field, enter the {linkto(../../../../vk-cloud-account/instructions/project-settings/manage#vk-cloud-account-project-pid)[text=PID]} of the customer's project, which can be {linkto(../../client-management/client-information#pp-client-information-view-projects)[text=viewed]} in the client information.
   1. (Optional) To add the PID of multiple projects, click **One more project**.
   1. Specify the deal amount with the customer excluding VAT.
   1. Select the expected year and quarter of the deal from the list.
   1. In the **Deal volume** field, specify the customer's expected expenses on resource consumption per year.
   1. In the **Deal description** field, describe in free form what the customer company does, which {var(cloud)} services it plans to use, and what it will host in the cloud.

1. Click the **Create** button.
1. In the window that appears, click **All applications** to go to the {linkto(../applications#pp-applications)[text=section]} **Applications** and make sure that your application has been successfully created.

Later, you can {linkto(../applications#pp-applications)[text=track]} the status of your application in the list of applications.
