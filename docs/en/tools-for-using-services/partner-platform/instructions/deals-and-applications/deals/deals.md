# {heading(Viewing deals)[id=pp-deals]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

1. Go to the [VK Tech partner management console](https://partners.tech.vk.ru).
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Cabinet** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Deal Registration** section.
1. Go to the **Confirmed Registrations** tab. A list of deals will open.
1. (Optional) Configure the list:

   1. Filter the list by deal status:

      - `All deals`: deals with any statuses. Applied by default.
      - `Successfully closed`: successfully completed deals.
      - `Failed`: unsuccessful deals. Contact your partner account manager to learn about the reasons.
      - `In progress`: deals currently being worked on by VK Tech.

   1. Use the text search by company name or INN (TIN) so that only deals with a specific company are displayed in the list.
   1. Select the number of deals displayed on the page from the drop-down list at the bottom of the page.
   1. Sort the list by deal number, status, or deal start date.

   The list of deals displays:

   - Deal number.
   - Name of the company with which the deal is concluded.
   - Company INN (TIN).
   - Deal status. Hover your mouse cursor over the status icon to view additional information.
   - Deal start date.
   - Deal closing date.

1. Click on the desired deal to view detailed information about it.
