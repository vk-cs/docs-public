## {heading(Inviting a client)[id=pp-invite-client-invite]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner management console.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Open the client addition form in one of the following ways:

   - On the main page, click **Add client**.
   - Go to the **Clients** section and click **Add**.

1. In the form that opens, fill in the fields:

   - **Client name**: the name that will be displayed in the partner management console and the client management console.
   - **Email**: the email address to which the invitation will be sent.
   - **Phone**: the client's phone number. Filled in if necessary.
   - **Identifier**: the client's unique identifier. You can take it, for example, from your CRM system where you register clients.

1. Select a payment model from the list: prepayment or postpayment. Later, you can {linkto(../client-management/payment-method#pp-payment-method)[text=change the payment model]} in the partner management console.
1. (For White Label partners) Enable the **Cloud Computing** service if you want the client to be able to use services without confirming a phone number.
1. Click the **Add** button.

An invitation will be sent to the specified email address. The client must {linkto(../accept-invitation#partner-platform-accept-invitation)[text=accept the invitation]} to join the partner project.

## {heading(Managing invitations)[id=pp-invite-client-control]}

### {heading(Resending an invitation)[id=pp-invite-client-again]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the partner management console.
1. Go to the **Clients** section.
1. Go to the **Invitations** tab.
1. Click ![ ](../../../../assets/more-icon.svg "inline") for the desired invitation and select **Send invite again**.

### {heading(Deleting an invitation)[id=pp-invite-client-delete]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the partner management console.
1. Go to the **Clients** section.
1. Go to the **Invitations** tab.
1. Click ![ ](../../../../assets/more-icon.svg "inline") for the desired invitation and select **Delete**.
