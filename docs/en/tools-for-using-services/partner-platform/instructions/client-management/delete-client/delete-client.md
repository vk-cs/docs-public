# {heading(Client deletion)[id=pp-client-delete]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner management console.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Clients** section.
1. Select one or more clients using checkboxes and click the **Delete** button.
1. Confirm the deletion.

After a client is deleted, the resources of all their projects will be deleted within three days. Clients with postpayment will be switched to prepayment immediately after you confirm the deletion.

If you want to delete one of the client's projects, use the {linkto(../../../../account/instructions/project-settings/manage#project-delete)[text=project deletion instructions]}.
