## {heading(Blocking a project)[id=pp-manage-project-block]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner management console.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Clients** section.
1. Click the name of the desired client in the list.
1. Go to the **Projects** tab.
1. Block the project in one of the following ways:

   - Click ![ ](../../../../../assets/more-icon.svg "inline") for the desired project and select **Block**.
   - Select one or more projects using the checkboxes and click the **Block** button.

1. Confirm the blocking.

Immediately after blocking, the client loses access to the blocked project, and resource consumption on the project stops.

{note:warn}
All resources on the blocked project will be deleted after three days.
{/note}

## {heading(Unblocking a project)[id=pp-manage-project-unblock]}

1. [Go to](https://partners.tech.vk.ru) the VK Tech partner management console.
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Clients** section.
1. Click the name of the desired client in the list.
1. Go to the **Projects** tab.
1. Unblock the project in one of the following ways:

   - Click ![ ](../../../../../assets/more-icon.svg "inline") for the desired project and select **Unblock**.
   - Select one or more projects using the checkboxes and click the **Unblock** button.

1. Confirm the unblocking.

If the project is unblocked within three days from the moment of blocking, all resources on the project will be preserved and available after unblocking.

{note:warn}
If the project is unblocked after three days, the client will gain access to the project, but the resources on it will be deleted.
{/note}
