## {heading(Viewing the list of clients)[id=pp-client-information-view-list]}

1. Go to the [VK Tech partner management console](https://partners.tech.vk.ru).
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Clients** section.
1. (Optional) Select the number of clients displayed on the page from the drop-down list at the bottom of the page.

The list of clients displays:

- the name under which the client registered;
- the client's email;
- the number of the client's projects;
- the client's registration date.

## {heading(Viewing detailed information about a client)[id=pp-client-information-view-info]}

1. Go to the [VK Tech partner management console](https://partners.tech.vk.ru).
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Clients** section.
1. Click the client's name.

The client information displays:

- the name under which the client registered;
- the client's ID;
- the client's email;
- the client's phone number;
- the client's registration date;
- the client's payment model: prepayment or postpayment.

## {heading(Viewing the list of a client's projects)[id=pp-client-information-view-projects]}

1. Go to the [VK Tech partner management console](https://partners.tech.vk.ru).
1. On the main page, click the **Partner Account** button in the {var(cloud)} card.
1. Go to the **Clients** section.
1. Click the client's name.
1. Go to the **Projects** tab.

The list of the client's projects displays:

- the project name;
- the project ID (PID).
