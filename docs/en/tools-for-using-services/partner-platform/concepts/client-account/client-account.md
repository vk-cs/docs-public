# {heading(Partner's client account)[id=partner-platform-client-account]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

The partner's client account contains the same tools for managing the {var(cloud)} user infrastructure as the {linkto(../../../account#tools-account)[text=personal user account]} of {var(cloud)}.

If you are a client of a [White Label](../../../../start/partners/cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models-agent-how) partner, you may have access to a limited set of {var(cloud)} services. Restrictions are set by the White Label partner who invited you to the platform.

A partner's client cannot top up their {linkto(../../../../intro/billing/concepts/balance#billing-balance)[text=balance]} in the management console; they pay for services directly to the partner. The partner, in turn, pays for the services received by the client from {var(cloud)}, minus their partner discount.

A partner's client has access to a section with {linkto(../../../../intro/billing/instructions/detail#billing-detail)[text=detailed reports]} on expenses and consumption. The partner sees the balance and consumption details of their clients in the {linkto(../../instructions/reports/balance#partner-platform-balance)[text=partner's management console]}. If a client's project bonus account has separate bonuses for virtual machines and for the other service groups, the partner also sees the remaining bonus amount and expiration date for each group of the bonus account.

The client's balance is not topped up automatically; funds are credited manually upon the partner's request. Service prices are listed in the [price list](https://cloud.vk.ru/pricelist), but if you are a client of a [White Label](../../../../start/partners/cooperation-models#partners-cooperation-models-agent-how) partner, your prices may differ from those listed.

A partner's clients receive a welcome {linkto(../../../../intro/billing/concepts/balance#billing-balance-bonuses)[text=bonus]} upon registration.
