# {heading(Partner management console)[id=partner-platform-ui]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

The partner management console of {var(cloud)} is an interface through which you can manage your clients' projects on the {var(cloud)} platform. The partner account is part of the VK Tech partner infrastructure.

The management console tools allow you to:

- invite clients to the platform;
- view the list of clients, their projects, and related information;
- check the current balance status of a client's project;
- obtain detailed breakdowns of the balance and consumption of client projects and export detailed reports;
- generate and export consumption reports for all of your clients;
- block a client's access to resources.
