# {heading(Publishing a service)[id=ibservice_upload_publish]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

The service will become available to consumers only after publication.

To publish an image-based application in the marketplace, send an email to [marketplace@cloud.vk.com](mailto:marketplace@cloud.vk.com). In the email, specify the service ID and its revision.

The service package will be moderated. After that, the service will be published in the marketplace.

The published revision of the service is moved from the test namespace of the marketplace to the public namespace specified in the service key. The service will be available to all users who have access to this public namespace.

{note:warn}

In public namespaces, only the last published revision of the service is available.

{/note}
