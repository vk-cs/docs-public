# {heading(Service publication)[id=saas_upload_publish]}

{include(/en/_includes/_translated_by_ai_en.md)}

The service becomes available to consumers only after publication.

To publish a SaaS application in the store, send an email to [marketplace@cloud.vk.com](mailto:marketplace@cloud.vk.com). In the email, specify the service ID and its revision.

The service configuration will be moderated. After that, the service will be published in the store.

The published service revision is moved from the store's test namespace to the public one specified in the `metadata` parameter in the service configuration. The service will be available to all users who have access to this public namespace.

{note:warn}

Only the last published revision of the service is available in public namespaces.

{/note}
