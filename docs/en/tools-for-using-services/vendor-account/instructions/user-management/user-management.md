You can add new users to your vendor account, assign them [roles](../../concepts/about#xaas_vendor_roles), and delete users when necessary.

## Viewing vendor account users

To view the list of users who are allowed to access your vendor account:

1. [Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/) the {var(cloud)} management console.
1. In the **App Store** section, click the **Go to vendor account** button.
1. Go to the **User Management** tab.

The user list displays their roles and the dates they were added to the account.

## Adding a user

To add a new user to your vendor account:

1. [Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/) the {var(cloud)} management console.
1. In the **App Store** section, click the **Go to vendor account** button.
1. On the **User Management** tab, click the **Add user** button.
1. In the **User email** field, enter the email of the user you want to add.
1. In the **Roles** field, specify the list of roles for the user.

    You can select multiple roles at once.

1. Click the **Add** button.

## Editing a user's role

The capabilities of vendor account users are determined by the [role model](../../concepts/about#xaas_vendor_roles). To change a user's role in your account:

1. [Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/) the {var(cloud)} management console.
1. In the **App Store** section, click the **Go to vendor account** button.
1. Go to the **User Management** tab.
1. Click ![ ](../../../../assets/more-icon.svg "inline") for the desired user and select **Edit roles**.
1. In the **User role** field, specify the desired roles.

    At least one role must remain in the list.

1. Click the **Save** button.
