In the vendor account, you can get reports on the usage of services hosted on the {var(cloud)} platform.

## Viewing reports

1. [Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/) the {var(cloud)} management console.
1. In the **Application Store** section, click the **Go to vendor account** button.
1. Go to the **Reports** tab.

The list will display all reports generated monthly for the entire period of using your services.

## Downloading reports

This is a group operation: if necessary, you can download several reports at once by selecting them using checkboxes.

1. [Go to](https://msk.cloud.vk.ru/app/) the {var(cloud)} management console.
1. In the **Application Store** section, click the **Go to vendor account** button.
1. Go to the **Reports** tab.
1. Download the desired report in one of the following ways:

   - Click ![ ](../../../../assets/more-icon.svg "inline") for the desired report and select **Download**.
   - Select the desired report using a checkbox and click the **Download** button.

1. Save the file to your device.
